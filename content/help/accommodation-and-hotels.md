## The hotel directory

Open **Hotels** from the main menu to see every hotel the Hub knows about. Search by name or city. Each row shows the hotel, city, contact details and how many bookings it has.

### Adding a hotel

1. Click **+ Add Hotel**.
2. Enter the **Name**, and add the **City**, **Phone**, **Address**, **Email**, **Website** and any **Notes**.
3. Click **Add Hotel**.

Use **Edit** to change a hotel. **Delete** removes the hotel and all its bookings on every production, so check first.

## Accommodation on a production

Open the production and choose **Accommodation**. The top of the section shows the **Total cost** of all bookings and how many are **Paid**.

### Adding a booking

1. Click **+ Add Booking**.
2. Pick the **Hotel**. Add it to the hotel directory first if it is not listed.
3. Choose who it is for in **Assign to Crew Member**. If the guest is not in the crew database, type their name in **Guest Name (if not in crew database)** instead. Leave both blank for a block booking.
4. Set the **Check In** and **Check Out** dates.
5. Add the **Room Type** (for example Double or Twin) and **Room Number** if you know it.
6. Enter the **Rate Per Night (£)** or the **Total Cost (£)**, and the **Confirmation / Ref No.**
7. Tick **Paid** if it is already paid, add any **Notes**, and click **Add Booking**.

### The bookings list

Each booking shows the guest, hotel, room, check-in and check-out dates, number of nights, total and reference.

If you only entered a nightly rate, the total is worked out from the rate and the number of nights. If you entered a total cost, that is used instead.

Click the **Paid** or **Unpaid** badge on a booking to switch it. Hover over a booking and click **Edit** to change it, or **×** to delete it.

## Exporting

Click **Export** and choose **Export PDF** or **Export Excel** for a list of the bookings.
