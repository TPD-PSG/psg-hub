## What aftercare is for

**Aftercare** in the TWS menu collects problems with sets that have left the workshop, such as a broken castor on a touring set. Reports come from two places:

- the TWS team, on this page
- clients, from their [My builds](/help/client-logins) page

The page is for PSG staff. Freelancers do not see it.

## Reporting a repair

1. Click **+ Report a repair**.
2. Pick the **Build**, and the **Piece** if it is one particular item.
3. Fill in **What needs repairing?** and any **Details**.
4. Say **Where is it now?**, for example the theatre it is in.
5. Choose **How urgent?**: **Urgent: the show needs it**, **Soon** or **Routine**.
6. Add a **Photo** if you have one. On a phone this can open the camera.
7. Click **Report it**.

When a repair is reported, by the team or by a client, the TWS team and the admins get a notification. Urgent ones say **URGENT repair**.

## Reading the list

Each report shows its urgency, what needs repairing, the build and piece, and its status. Underneath you see where it is, who reported it (marked "(client)" if it came from the client), the date and the client's name. **Open the photo** shows any photo sent with it.

Use the tabs to filter: **Open**, **Booked in**, **Done** or **All**.

Click the piece name to open the piece's page, with where it is stored and its drawings. See [Set storage and QR labels](/help/storage-and-labels).

## Moving a repair on

Repairs go from open, to booked in, to done.

- **Book it in**: pick the date it is **Booked in for** and add a **Plan** if you like, then click **Book it in**. Use **Change date** to move it.
- **Mark done**: say **What was done?** and click **Mark done**.
- **Reopen**: puts a done repair back to open.

If the client reported the repair, they are told when it is booked in and when it is done. They can follow it in My builds.

Only admins can **Delete** a report.
