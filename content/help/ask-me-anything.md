## What it does

**Ask Me Anything** answers questions about company policies, such as "What is the expenses policy?" or "How much annual leave do I get?". It reads the documents in the **Knowledge Base** and answers from them. If the answer is not in the documents, it should say so rather than guess.

Open it from **Help and ideas**, then **Ask me anything**.

## Who can use it

- Staff with the **Ask** module, and all freelancers, can ask questions. Clients cannot.
- Only admins can add or remove documents.

## Asking a question

1. Type your question in the box at the bottom.
2. Press Enter or click **Ask**. The answer appears after a few seconds, while **Thinking…** shows.
3. Ask follow-up questions in the same conversation. It remembers what you have already asked.
4. Click **Start new conversation** to clear it and begin again.

Conversations are not saved. If you leave or reload the page, the conversation is gone, so copy anything you need first.

The answers come from an AI service. Treat them as a quick guide, and check the policy document itself or ask your manager before acting on anything important, such as pay, leave or disciplinary matters.

## Cost and limits

Every question sends the whole Knowledge Base to the AI service, and PSG pays for each question. Follow-up questions in the same conversation cost less. Please use it for real questions rather than testing.

Each person can ask up to 30 questions an hour. After that, you are asked to try again later.

## Adding documents (admins)

The **Knowledge Base** panel on the left lists every document and the date it was added.

1. Click **+ Add**, or drag a file onto the box marked **Drop PDF or text file here**.
2. Choose the policy document: a PDF, or a plain text file (.txt or .md). Save a Word or Excel file as a PDF first. If a file can't be added, the Hub says why.
3. It appears in the list straight away and is used for the next question.

Click the cross beside a document to remove it from the Knowledge Base. You are asked to confirm.

Keep the Knowledge Base to current policies only. Old versions can lead to wrong answers. If the library grows too large, questions stop working until an admin removes or splits some documents.

For questions about how the Hub works, use the [Guides](/help) or the [Help desk](/help/help-desk) instead.
