## Who can use it

The pipeline is for admins and staff with the **BD** module. You only see deals for the companies you have access to. Only admins can delete a deal.

## Adding a deal

1. Go to **BD pipeline** and choose **+ Opportunity**.
2. Give it a **Title**. Everything else is optional: **Type**, **Stage**, **Value (£)**, **Close date**, **Client**, **Entity** (which company it is for), **Owner**, **Lead source** and **Competitor**.
3. Choose **Add Opportunity**.

If you leave **Owner** as **Me**, the deal is yours.

## Stages and weighted value

Deals move through **Lead**, **Qualified**, **Proposal**, **Negotiating**, then **Won** or **Lost**. Each open stage counts a share of the deal's value: 10%, 25%, 50% and 75%. If you know better, type your own figure in **Chance of winning (%)** and that is used instead.

The header shows the full pipeline, the weighted pipeline and the value won. When you mark a deal **Won** or **Lost**, you can note **Why we won** or **Why we lost**.

## Working a deal

Click a deal to open its panel. From there you can:

- Click a stage to move it. Each move is logged.
- **Log** a note, call, email or meeting, and set the **Next follow-up** date.
- Add **Follow-up tasks** with due dates.
- Start a quote with **+ Quote**. See [Quotes](/help/quotes).

## Deals that need attention

A deal needs attention when its follow-up date has passed, or when it has no follow-up date and nothing has been logged for 21 days. These deals have an amber outline and a warning. Choose **Needs attention** to see only these.

## Tender deadlines

Set a **Tender deadline** on a deal you are bidding for. The card shows it, turning red in the last three days. The owner is reminded 7, 3 and 1 days before, and on the day.

## When a deal is won

- **→ Production** creates a production from the deal, with its PM fee and payment stages.
- **→ Job** opens a job for work that is not production management. See [Jobs and costing](/help/jobs-and-costing).

## Views and filters

- **Pipeline** shows a column per stage with its weighted total.
- **List** shows every deal and lets you filter by stage.
- **Results** shows win rate, average won deal and days to close for deals decided in a chosen period, by company, owner and lead source.

Filter by entity or owner at the top. Clients are listed below the pipeline. See [Clients](/help/clients).

## Targets

Admins set each company's yearly revenue target in **Hub Settings** under **Entities & revenue targets**. The forecast on the [Director dashboard](/help/director-dashboard) compares confirmed and weighted pipeline revenue with these targets.
