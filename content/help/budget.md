## Who sees the budget

By default, clients and freelancers do not see **Budget**. The **PM fee** panel at the top is for PSG staff only.

## The four figures

- **Allocation:** the money each line has, including approved reallocations.
- **PM Budget:** your estimate of what it will cost.
- **Net Cost:** what is committed, such as orders placed.
- **Gross Cost:** what has been invoiced.

Switch between **Detail** and **Summary**. Summary is one table by department with a **Variance** column (allocation less net cost).

## Departments and lines

1. Click **+ Add Department**.
2. Type or pick a **Department**, then add a **Description**, **Nominal Code**, **Overall Allocation (£)** and **Notes**. Click **Add**.

To add another line to a department, use the same button and pick the same department. Drag a department to reorder. Use its **Edit** to rename it, or **Delete** to remove it with all its lines.

Click a line to open its spend. Bars turn orange at 75% and red at 100%.

## Recording spend

Click **+ Spend** on a line. Fill in the description, supplier, phase, **PM Budget (£)**, **Net Cost (£)**, PO number and payment due date. Drop files into **Quotes**, **Purchase Order** and **Invoices**. Invoice details are read from PDFs and images automatically. Gross cost is the total of the invoices. Click **Add Spend**.

Haulage movements linked to a line count towards it. See [Haulage](/help/haulage).

## PSG expenses

Click **+ PSG Expense** for money PSG paid itself. Add the costs, payment date and method, and a **Receipt / Invoice**.

## Reallocations

Under **Budget Reallocation**, click **+ New Request**. Choose **Reallocation**, **Additional Spend** or **Saving**. Add a **Title** and reason. Add each budget line and amount (negative for lines losing money). Click **Save as Draft**, then **Submit for Approval**. An admin or the client clicks **Approve** or **Reject**. Approved amounts go into the lines' allocations.

## PM fee

Click **+ Add PM fee** (or **Edit**). Enter the fee and add payment stages with amounts and due, invoiced and paid dates. The panel shows what is paid, invoiced and still to invoice.

## Overspend alerts

A banner lists lines that are **Nearly spent** (90% or more) or **Over**, measured on net cost. When a line or the whole budget first reaches 90% or goes over, PSG staff on the show's team get a Hub notification. If nobody is on the team, the admins get it. Each alert is sent once.

## Exports and the client report

Click **Export** and choose **Export PDF** or **Export Excel**. Choose **Client report (PDF)…** for a client version with department and line totals, variations and rechargeables, but no suppliers or POs. Add an optional covering note and click **Create PDF**.
