## The stage band

At the top is a row of the six stages. Click one to move the build to it.

## Drawings and sign-off

1. In **Drawings & sign-off**, click **+ Add a drawing**, give it a number and title, and upload a PDF or image. It becomes rev A.
2. When the drawing changes, click **New revision**. The older revision is marked Superseded and only the latest one counts.
3. The client signs off in [My builds](/help/client-logins), or asks for changes.

While any latest revision is not approved, the build cannot move to Build, Finish or Delivery. An admin can pick drawings and use **Record approval** or **Record changes** when the client answered by email or phone. An admin can also override the block with a reason, which is logged. **History** shows every revision and decision.

## Parts and materials

**Parts of this build** splits the work into pieces, such as flats and doors. Set who, labour type, start date, days and hours, then click **Add part**. Tick a part when it is done.

Click **+ Materials** under a part to list its estimated materials. Parts added at the Quote stage are marked **Pencilled**. **+ Add from a template** and **Save as template** reuse typical work.

In **Materials & purchase orders**, click **Order from the estimates** to raise one purchase order, or book lines out of stock. See [Materials and stock](/help/materials-and-stock).

## Design and contacts

**Design & contacts** holds contacts, reference drawings and model box photos. **Drawings & files** holds VectorWorks links and the build's quotes.

## Days, photos and snags

- **Days logged**: record days worked against the build.
- **Build photos**: click **+ Add photos**. **Share** puts a photo on the noticeboard. Tick **Client** to show it in the client's progress photos.
- **Snags & tasks**: add items and tick them off.

## Collection or delivery

The dark **Collection** or **Delivery** card shows the due-out date. Click **Edit** to change dates, method and haulier notes. Add ready-to-collect checks, or click **+ Add the standard ready-to-collect checks**. **Collection pack (PDF)** makes a load list with weights, FR certificates, drawings and the checks.

## FR certificates

In **FR certificates**, click **+ Add a certificate** and upload it with what it covers, the standard, supplier and dates. Expired ones are flagged.

## Quote vs actual and variations

Only people who can see money see these. **Quote vs actual** shows price, spend and margin, and **Quote this build** (see [TWS quotes](/help/tws-quotes)). **Variations** price extra work after the quote. Click **+ New variation**, add lines, then **Send to client**. The client approves or declines in My builds, or an admin records their answer. Approved variations add to the price.
