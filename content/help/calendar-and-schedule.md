## Opening them

In the new look, open **Calendar and schedule** in the sidebar, then **Calendar** or **Schedule**. In the old look, use the **Calendar** and **Schedule** tiles on Home.

Both pages show every production you can see. If you have team only access, that means the shows you are on. You need the Productions area to use them.

Nothing is entered on these pages directly, apart from staff bookings. Dates come from each production's key dates, tour stops, performance schedule and tasks. To change a date, change it on the production.

## The calendar

The calendar shows:

- key dates, such as fit-up, tech, press night and get-out
- tour stops, with load in, performances and get out at each venue
- performances, marked as evening or matinee
- tasks on their due date

Use **‹** and **›** to change month, and **Today** to come back to this month.

Choose a view at the top right:

- **Month**: a grid of the month. Click a day to see everything on it. A day with more than three items shows how many more there are.
- **List**: everything in the month in date order. Click an item to open it.
- **Schedule**: who is working on what, day by day.

### Showing only some things

In the old look, the sidebar on the calendar page lists your productions and the types of event. Click a production or a type to hide or show it. Click **Show all** to bring everything back.

## Staff bookings

The calendar's **Schedule** view has a column for each production with activity that month. Each day shows the production's activity and the staff booked on it, in each person's schedule colour.

To book someone:

1. Click **+ Assign Staff**, or click the **+** in a day's staff cell.
2. Choose the **Staff member** and **Production**.
3. Set **From** and **To**, and an **Activity type** if you want one.
4. Add **Notes** if needed, and save.

Click a person's name in the grid to change or delete their booking. You can only book people onto productions you have access to. Staff bookings are for PSG staff; freelancers don't see them.

Admins set each person's schedule colour in **Admin → Users**.

## The schedule page

**Schedule** lists every day with something on, across all your productions, with today highlighted.

- Performances show the production and venue, with **Evening**, **+ Matinee** or **Matinee only**. Click one to open the production.
- Timed events, such as rehearsals, tech and meetings, show their start and end times, type and location. Click the production name to open it.

Add these from the production's **Schedule** section.
