## Making a call sheet

Open the production and choose **Call sheets**. There is one sheet per day, listed on the left. Use **Edit** or **Delete** at the top of a sheet to change or remove it.

1. Click **+ New Call Sheet**.
2. Pick the **Date**. Add a **Title**, such as "Get In Day 1" or "Show Day".
3. Leave **Venue** blank to use that day's venue: the tour stop covering the day, or the show's own venue.
4. Add any **General Notes**.
5. Leave **Fill in the crew and PSG staff booked that day** ticked to add people straight away.
6. Click **Create Call Sheet**.

## Who gets filled in

The sheet adds crew whose dates on the [Crew](/help/crew) section cover that day. Crew with no dates count as booked for the whole run. It also adds PSG staff assigned to the show that day, under the department **PSG**.

If bookings change, click **Add people booked that day**. It only adds people not already on the sheet.

## The day's schedule

The sheet shows the events from [Schedule](/help/schedule-and-key-dates) that start that day, with their times and locations. To change them, edit the schedule.

## Calls by call time

Everyone is in one list, sorted by call time. Rows without a time show **TBC**.

- Click **+ Add Row** to add a person or a whole department. Fill in the **Department**, and a **Name** and **Role** if it is a person. Leave the name blank for a department call, such as all of Lighting.
- Set the **Call Time** and any **Notes**.
- For someone not on the Crew section or the Hub, add an **Email** so they can be sent the sheet.
- Click **Edit** on a row to change it, or **✕** to remove it.

A **no email** tag means the person has no address on the row or on their record. They will not be emailed.

## PDF and email

Click **Download PDF** for a printable sheet.

PSG staff can click **Email to crew**. The Hub tells you how many people it will reach and who has no email, then asks you to confirm. Each person gets the PDF with their own call time in the email. Department rows are not emailed.

The sheet then shows when it went, to how many people, and any that failed.

## Updates

If you change a sheet after sending it, it shows **changed since sent** and the button becomes **Send update**. The next email and PDF are marked **UPDATED**. If nothing has changed, the button reads **Send again**.

If email is not set up on the Hub, download the PDF and send it yourself.
