## Setting up a client login

An admin adds the person in Admin → Users, sets their role to **Client** and picks their **Client company**. A client login sees that company's builds (and hires) and nothing else in the Hub.

When a client logs in they see **Your builds**. The page is in the builder's own brand, so a TWS build shows the TWS logo and colours.

If PSG Rentals & Crew also hires kit to them, they get a **Hires** tab too: see [Rentals & Crew](/help/rentals-and-hires#client-logins). A client who only hires kit lands on Hires when they sign in.

## What the client sees on each build

- The build's dates and a **Progress** strip of its six stages, with ticks on the stages already done.
- **Drawings** waiting for their sign-off.
- **Variations** sent to them.
- **Repairs & aftercare** they have reported.
- **Progress photos** the team have chosen to share.

## Signing off drawings

Each drawing shows its latest revision and its status: **Awaiting your sign-off**, **Approved** or **Changes requested**. Click **Open drawing** to view it.

To decide on one drawing:

- click **Approve rev** and the revision letter, or
- click **Request changes**, say what needs changing, and click **Send back**.

To decide on several at once, tick them, or click **Pick all not yet approved**. A bar appears with **Approve all** and **Request changes**.

**History** shows every revision and decision. The workshop is told about each decision, and the client about each new drawing or revision. The build cannot go into the workshop until every latest revision is approved. See [Build jobs](/help/build-jobs).

## Progress photos

On a build's page, the team tick **Client** on a photo to share it. Only those photos show under **Progress photos**.

## Approving variations

A variation is extra or changed work priced after the quote. The client sees each line's description, quantity and price, and the totals ex VAT and with VAT. They never see our costs.

- Click **Approve** with the variation number, such as **Approve V1**, or
- click **Decline**, say why, and click **Decline** again.

**Download as PDF** saves a copy. Once decided, it shows who approved or declined it and when. If the team recorded the answer for them, it says so.

## Reporting a repair

1. On the build, under **Repairs & aftercare**, click **Report a problem**.
2. Fill in **What's wrong?**, and pick **Which piece?** if you know.
3. Add detail, **Where is the set now?** and **How urgent?**
4. Add a photo if you have one, then click **Send to** and the builder's name.

The workshop is told straight away. The client sees the status change from **Reported** to **Booked in** to **Done**, and gets a notification at each step. See [Aftercare and repairs](/help/aftercare-and-repairs).
