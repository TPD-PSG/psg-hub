## Who can use clients

Clients live in Business development. Admins and staff with the **BD** module can add and edit them. Staff with the **Productions** module can look them up. Only admins can delete a client.

## Adding a client

1. Go to **BD pipeline** and choose **+ Client**.
2. Enter the **Name**. Pick a **Type** (promoter, venue, council, broadcaster, corporate or other) and add a **Website** and **Notes** if useful.
3. Fill in **Billing Details** if you have them: **Billing Name** (the legal entity), **Billing Address** and **Billing Email**.
4. Choose **Add Client**.

The billing details go on the client box of quote PDFs. If they are blank, the client's name and address are used instead.

## The clients list

The list sits under the pipeline. Each client shows its type, how many open deals it has and who to bill. Choose **Edit** to change its details.

Target accounts are marked with a star and listed first. Tick **Target accounts only** to see just those.

## A client's page

Click a client's name to open its page. It brings everything together:

- **Open deals** with their weighted value, deals **Won**, and **Productions**.
- **Deals**: each deal with its stage, value, owner and next follow-up. Deals that need attention are shown in amber.
- **Contacts**: choose **+ Add** to add a person with their role, email and phone. Hover over a contact to **Remove** them.
- **Productions** for this client, with the PM fee.
- **Sales invoices**, for people with the **Finance** module. These are matched where the invoice's client name is the same as the client's name or billing name, so keep the spelling the same.

## Target accounts

A target account is a client we want to win work from.

1. Open the client's page.
2. Choose **Make target** (or **Edit** if it is already one).
3. Tick **Target account**, choose an owner and note where the lead came from.
4. Choose **Save**.

In the list, a target account with no open deal is flagged in amber, so nobody forgets to follow it up.

To start work with a client, add a deal for them. See [BD pipeline](/help/bd-pipeline).
