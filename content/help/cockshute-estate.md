## Who can use it

The estate pages are for people with the **Property** module. Admins always have it. Anyone who can open the pages can add, edit and delete records here, so take care when deleting.

Open it from **Operations**, then **Cockshute Industrial Estate**. The side menu has five pages: **Overview**, **Maintenance**, **Documents**, **Contacts** and **Keys & Alarms**.

## Warnings at the top

A red strip appears above every page when something needs attention. It lists any urgent maintenance issue that is not resolved, and any document that has expired or expires within 90 days.

## Overview

The **Rent** card shows the amount and how often it is paid, the next payment date, the rent review date and the landlord reference. Click **Edit** to change them.

Beside it are four counts: **Open issues**, **Urgent**, **Docs expiring soon** and **Contacts**.

## Maintenance

Use this to log faults and jobs on the estate.

1. Click **+ Add**. The **Log Issue** form opens.
2. Give it a title. Add a description, the contractor, the cost and notes if you have them.
3. Pick a priority: low, normal, high or urgent. New issues start as **Reported**.
4. Click **Save**.

Each issue has quick buttons. **Start** moves a reported issue to **In Progress**. **Resolve** marks it **Resolved** and records the date. Use **Edit** to change anything else.

## Documents

Keep the estate's certificates here: lease, gas safety, EICR, insurance, PAT test, fire risk assessment, asbestos survey and others.

Click **+ Add**, give the document a name and type, and add its expiry date. The **File URL** box takes a link to where the file is stored, such as SharePoint. The Hub does not store the file itself here. Click **View file** on a document to open the link.

Documents are listed with the soonest expiry first. A badge shows when one expires within 90 days, turns orange inside 30 days, and shows **Expired** once the date has passed.

## Contacts

Add the landlord, agents, contractors and anyone else you call about the estate. Each contact needs a name and a role. Phone, email and notes are optional.

## Keys & Alarms

**Keys** records each key or padlock, who holds it and how many there are.

**Alarms** records each system, such as the intruder or fire alarm. It holds the code, zones, monitoring company and phone number, and the keyholders. Alarm codes are for admins only: everyone else sees the alarm records with "Code: admins only", and only admins can add, edit or delete alarms.

Company-wide insurances and certificates belong on the [Compliance register](/help/compliance-register). Estate contractors you use often can also go in the [Supplier register](/help/suppliers).
