## What goes on the register

The register holds the group's insurances, accreditations, policies, certificates and registrations. Each item belongs to one company or to **The whole group**, and has a renewal or review date, an owner and its documents.

Open it from **Operations**, then **Compliance register**.

## Who can see and change it

- Admins and staff members can see the register. Freelancers and clients cannot.
- If your account is limited to certain companies, you see their items plus the group-wide ones.
- Only admins add items, change an item's owner or company, retire items and delete documents.
- An item's owner can update its details and dates and upload its documents.
- Anyone who can see an item can download its documents.

## Reading the register

The counts at the top show **Items**, **Expired**, **Due in 60 days** and **No date set**. Each row has a status: **In date**, **Due soon** (within 60 days), **Expired** or **No date**.

Filter by company, by kind, or with **Search**. Tick **Show retired** to see items no longer in use.

## Adding an item (admins)

1. Click **+ Add to the register**.
2. Enter the name, such as Public liability insurance, and pick the kind.
3. Under **Belongs to**, pick the company or leave it as **The whole group**.
4. Add who issued it, the policy or certificate number and the cover or level. For a policy, add the version instead.
5. Pick an owner to keep it up to date, or leave it as **Admins**.
6. Add the dates and click **Add**. The item opens so you can upload the document.

## Documents and renewals

Click an item's name to open it. Click **Upload the document**, choose a PDF, Word file or picture (20 MB at most), and add the dates it covers.

When the renewal arrives, click **Upload the renewal**. Leave **This is the renewal: move the item's dates to these** ticked. The new document becomes current and the item's dates move on. Older documents stay listed as history.

## Reminders

The owner and every admin get a Hub notification 60, 30 and 7 days before a renewal or review date, and on the day. If an item is overdue, they are reminded each Monday until it is renewed.

To stop reminders for something you no longer hold, an admin opens it and clicks **Retire**. Its documents are kept, and **Bring back** restores it.

## Tender pack

Tick items in the list and click **Tender pack PDF**. You get a PDF listing those items, their numbers, cover and dates. Download the documents themselves from each item.

Estate certificates such as gas safety and EICR are kept on [Cockshute Industrial Estate](/help/cockshute-estate).
