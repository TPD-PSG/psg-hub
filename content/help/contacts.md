## The contact sheet

Open the production and choose **Contacts**. This is the show's contact sheet: the creative team, producers, venue staff, suppliers and anyone else people need to reach. Contacts are grouped into sections, such as Producers or Wardrobe.

## Adding a contact

1. Click **+ Add Contact**.
2. Pick or type a **Section**.
3. Enter the **Name**, and add their **Role / Title**, **Company**, **Phone**, **Email** and any **Notes**.
4. Click **Add Contact**.

Use **Edit** on a contact to change it, or **✕** to delete it.

## Sections

Click **Sections** to choose which sections the sheet uses.

- **This production's sections** are shown on the contact sheet even before anyone is in them. Type a name, such as "Wardrobe", and click **Add**. Removing a section here does not delete its contacts.
- **Company defaults** are offered on every production. Click one to add it to this show. Admins change the default list in [Hub Settings](/help/hub-settings).

To change the order, drag a section or a contact by its **⠿** handle and drop it in place. The new order saves straight away and is used on the PDF.

## Importing a contact sheet

If you already have a contact sheet from another company, the Hub can read it for you.

1. Click **↑ Import**, or drop the file onto the box that says **Drop a contact sheet here**. PDF, Excel, Word and CSV files all work.
2. Wait while the Hub reads the document. The button shows **Analysing…**.
3. Check the list under **Review imported contacts**. Each contact shows its name, role, company, section, phone and email. Anyone who looks like a contact you already have is marked **already exists**.
4. Untick anyone you don't want.
5. Click **Import** (the button shows how many contacts) to add them, or **Cancel** to stop.

Nothing is added until you click **Import**. If the Hub finds nobody in the file, it says so.

## Exporting the contact sheet

Click **↓ Export PDF** to download the contact sheet with the production's logo, laid out by section in your chosen order. Send it to the company or print it for the prompt desk.
