## The crew directory

Open **Crew** from the main menu to see everyone in the crew database. The count at the top shows how many people there are.

Search by name, discipline or company, or pick a discipline from the list to narrow it down. Each row shows the person's discipline, contact details, company, insurance and the productions they are booked on. People marked **Freelance** have a small badge.

### Adding someone

1. Click **+ Add Crew Member**.
2. Under **Personal Details**, enter their **Name** and pick a **Discipline**. Add their **Phone**, **Email** and **Company**, and tick **Freelance** if they work for themselves.
3. Under **Insurance**, add the **Provider**, **Policy Number**, **Expiry Date** and **Cover Type** (for example PLI or Employers Liability).
4. Add any **Notes** and click **Add Member**.

Hover over a row and click **Edit** to change someone, or **Delete** to remove them from the database.

### Insurance warnings

Insurance shows as **Valid**, **Expiring** (within 60 days) or **Expired**. Banners at the top of the directory count expired policies and those expiring within 60 days, so you can chase them before people go on site.

## Crew on a production

Open the production and choose **Crew**. By default, clients and freelancers do not see this section.

### Booking someone onto the show

1. Click **+ Add Crew Member**.
2. Pick the **Crew Member** from the database. Add them to the directory first if they are new.
3. Add their **Role on this Production**. Leave it blank to use their discipline.
4. Set the **Start Date** and **End Date** they are booked for. Leave the dates blank if they are on the whole run.
5. Add a **Day Rate (£)** and any **Notes**, then click **Add to Production**.

The table shows each person's role, dates, day rate, contact details and insurance status. Hover over a row and click **Remove** to take them off the show. This does not delete them from the directory.

### Where bookings are used

The dates you set here decide who is filled in on a [call sheet](/help/call-sheets) for each day, and who shows in **Crew on this week** on the [Overview](/help/production-overview). Crew can also be assigned to [hotel bookings](/help/accommodation-and-hotels).

## Exporting

On both pages, click **Export** and choose **Export PDF** or **Export Excel** for a crew list. In the directory, the export follows your search and filter.
