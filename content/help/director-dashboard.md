## Who can see it

The **Director dashboard** is for admins only. Open it from **Admin** in the menu. Anyone else who tries the address is sent back to Home.

## Revenue forecast

The top panel compares each company's revenue for the year with its target.

- **Confirmed** is PM fee stages by their due date, plus won deals that have not yet been made into a production.
- **Weighted pipeline** is each open deal's weighted value, placed in the month it is expected to close. See [BD pipeline](/help/bd-pipeline).
- **Still to find** (or **Ahead of target**) is the target minus confirmed and pipeline.

Switch between **By entity**, with a bar per company and a white mark for its target, and **By month**. Use the arrows to look at another year. Targets are set by admins in **Hub Settings** under **Entities & revenue targets**.

## The rest of the page

- **Finance**: sales raised, collected and outstanding, and purchases to pay, with a list of overdue sales invoices.
- **Productions**: active shows, open and overdue tasks, and expenses waiting for approval. Click a show to open it.
- **Business Dev Pipeline**: open deals, their total value, and deals won and lost.
- **Help Desk** and **Overdue Tasks** appear when there is something to show.

Each section has **View all** to jump to the full page.

## Company rows on Home

The Home page shows one row per company in the group for PSG staff. Each part only appears if you can see that area, and members only see their own companies and shows.

- **Live shows**: productions that are not closed, with links to the first few.
- **Pipeline**: the weighted value of open deals. A warning shows how many deals need attention.
- **Out this week**: how many crew and staff are booked on that company's shows this week, Monday to Sunday.
- **Next 14 days**: openings, closings, load-ins and tour stops coming up.
- **Waiting**: budget changes to approve, expenses to approve, and PM fee stages due to be invoiced.

If a company has its own space in the Hub, its name links to that space. Shows and deals with no company set are grouped under **No entity**. Admins can fix this by giving them a company.
