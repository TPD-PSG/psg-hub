## Documents

Open the production and choose **Documents**. This holds all documents for the production: drawings, schedules, riders, plans and anything else the team needs.

### Folders

Every production starts with a **General** folder. Click a folder's name to open or close it.

To make a new folder:

1. Click **+ New Folder**.
2. Choose where it goes: **Top level**, or **Inside:** an existing folder to make a sub-folder.
3. Type the **Folder name**. The box underneath shows the full path it will create.
4. Click **Create**, or press Enter.

The new folder opens ready for files.

### Uploading files

Drop files onto a folder's upload box, or click the box that says **Drop files here or click to upload** and pick them. You can upload several at once. Click a file to open or download it.

### Budget Documents

If quotes, purchase orders or invoices have been attached to spend on the [Budget](/help/budget), they also appear here in a **Budget Documents** folder marked **auto-populated**. It has sub-folders for **Quotes**, **Purchase Orders**, **Invoices** and **Other**. You don't upload to this folder; it fills itself from the budget.

Health and safety files have their own place. See [Health and safety](/help/health-and-safety).

## Links

Open **Links** for the show's online folders and tools, such as the Dropbox, a Google Drive folder, a Notion page or a WeTransfer. Links keep everything in one place so nobody has to search their email for the right address.

By default, clients and freelancers do not see Links.

### Adding a link

1. Click **+ Add Link**.
2. Enter a **Title**, for example "Show Dropbox Folder".
3. Paste the **URL**.
4. Add a **Description** if it helps, then click **Add Link**.

The Hub recognises common services, including Dropbox, OneDrive, SharePoint, Google Drive, Notion, GitHub, Figma, Slack, WeTransfer, Trello and Airtable, and labels the link to match.

Click a link to open it in a new tab. Use **Edit** to change it, or **×** to remove it.
