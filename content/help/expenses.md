## Who sees what

Expenses sit in the **Expenses** tab of Finance, so you need the **Finance** module (admins always have it).

- Staff see only the claims they submitted.
- Admins see everyone's claims, and are the only people who can approve, reject or mark a claim paid.

## Submitting a claim

1. Go to **Finance** and open the **Expenses** tab.
2. Choose **+ Expense**.
3. Fill in the **Description**, such as "Van hire for get-out". This is the only required box.
4. Pick a **Category** and, if it was for a show, the **Production**.
5. Add the **Supplier**, a **Reference / Receipt** number, the **Net Amount** and **VAT**, and the **Date**.
6. Choose a **Payment Method**: **To Pay** if you are owed the money, or **Card**, **Cash** or **Transfer** to record how it was paid.
7. Choose **Add Expense**.

Every new claim starts as **Pending**.

## Adding receipts

Click the claim in the list to open its panel. Under **Invoices & Receipts**:

1. Pick **Receipt**, **Invoice** or **Other** from the small menu.
2. Choose **+ Upload document** and select a photo or PDF, up to 20 MB.

You can add several files. Click a file name to open it, or the cross to remove it. Only you and admins can see the files on your claim.

The Hub does not read receipts for you, so type the amounts in yourself.

## Changing or withdrawing a claim

While a claim is **Pending** you can **Edit** or **Delete** it from its panel. Once it has been approved, rejected or paid, only an admin can change it.

## Approving and paying (admins)

Open a claim to see who submitted it, the totals and the receipts.

- Choose **Approve** on a pending claim.
- Choose **Mark Paid** once an approved claim has been paid.
- To reject a claim, click **Rejected** in the status buttons at the top of the panel. The person who submitted it sees that it was rejected.

Use the status buttons above the list (**Pending**, **Approved**, **Paid**, **Rejected**) to filter. Pending claims can also appear on the Home page, in the list of things waiting for you.

Claims linked to a production also show in the **Production Expenses** tab, where finance can tick them off once they have been recharged. See [Finance](/help/finance).
