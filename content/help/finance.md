## Who can use Finance

Finance is open to admins and to staff whose account has the **Finance** module. The line under the page title shows what is outstanding from clients, what we owe suppliers, unpaid expenses and how many invoices are overdue.

The page has five tabs: **Sales Invoices**, **Purchase Invoices**, **Expenses**, **Invoice To-Do** and **Production Expenses**. Expenses has its own guide: [Expenses](/help/expenses).

## Sales and purchase invoices

1. Open the right tab and choose **+ Sales Invoice** or **+ Purchase Invoice**.
2. Fill in the client or supplier, a description, and the net and VAT amounts. Reference, production, issue date, due date and notes are optional.
3. Choose **Add Invoice**.

Sales invoices move through **Draft**, **Sent**, **Overdue** and **Paid**. Purchase invoices move through **Received**, **Overdue** and **Paid**. Use the status buttons above the list to filter.

Click an invoice to open its panel. From there you can change its status, choose **Mark Paid**, **Edit** it or **Delete** it. A due date that has passed on an unpaid invoice shows in red.

A client's page in Business development lists the sales invoices whose client name matches that client. See [Clients](/help/clients).

## Invoice To-Do

This is a list of invoices that still need raising, so nothing is forgotten.

1. Choose **+ Add**.
2. Enter the client and what the invoice is for. Add a production, amount, **Due by** date and notes if you have them.
3. Choose **Add**.

Tick the box when the invoice has been raised. Hover over a row to **Edit** or **Remove** it. You only see items for productions in companies you can see.

## Production Expenses

This tab gathers spend on productions that may need recharging to the client:

- **PSG Expenses**: budget items marked as a PSG expense, with committed and spent amounts and whether they have been issued for payment.
- **Rechargeable Haulage**: haulage movements marked rechargeable, with estimated and actual cost.
- **Production Expenses**: expense claims linked to a production.

Filter by production, type or a search word. Tick **Not yet invoiced** to see only what still needs billing. Tick the **Invoiced** box on a row once it has gone on a client invoice.

Click a PSG expense or haulage row for its details and a link back to the production's budget or logistics. Use the export menu to download the filtered list as a PDF or Excel file.
