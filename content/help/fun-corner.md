## What it is for

**Fun Corner**, headed **This is not a place of entertainment.**, collects the moments, mishaps and mayhem from working at PSG. Stories are kept private during the month and revealed to everyone in a monthly roundup.

Open it from **Fun Corner** in the menu. You need the **Fun** module. Admins and freelancers always have it.

## Build of the month

At the top you may see a card for each company space, such as The Workshop Stoke, that runs a build of the month. It shows last month's winner and how this month's vote is going. Click **Vote** to go to that space's noticeboard and cast your vote, or **Change your vote** if you already have.

## Adding a story

1. Click **+ Add something funny**.
2. Give it a **Headline**. This is the only thing you must fill in.
3. Write **The story** if you want to.
4. Add **Photos / Videos** by dropping them onto the box or clicking it. JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, HEIC, MP4, MOV and WebM are accepted, up to 150 MB per file.
5. Click **Submit**.

Your story goes into this month's roundup. Stories added from inside a company space carry a **From** badge with that company's name.

## Before the roundup

Your story shows under **Your pending submissions** until the roundup is sent. Only you and the admins can see it. Click the cross on it to remove it. Once a story is in a roundup, only an admin can remove it.

Please keep stories kind. Check that anyone in a photo or video is happy for it to be shared.

## The monthly roundup (admins)

Admins see **All pending submissions** for the month.

1. Read each story. Click **Approve** on the ones to include. Click it again to undo.
2. Click **Delete** to remove a story that should not go in. This cannot be undone.
3. The **Send Roundup** panel shows how many stories are approved. Click **Send Roundup** and confirm.

Approved stories from the month go in, along with any approved stories from earlier months that missed their own roundup. Stories you have not approved stay pending. Everyone with a Hub login gets a notification that the roundup is out. Sending cannot be undone.

## Past roundups

**Past Roundups** lists every roundup sent, newest first, with its number of stories. The latest one opens on its own. Click any month to open or close it and see its stories, photos and videos.
