## Signing in

Go to the Hub and enter your **Email** and **Password**, then click **Sign in**.

If you were invited, you will have an email called "You've been invited to PSG Hub". Click **Accept Invitation**, choose a password of at least 8 characters, and click **Activate account**. The link lasts 7 days. If it has run out, ask an admin to send a new one.

Forgotten your password? Click **Get help** on the sign-in page. An admin can set a new one for you in **Admin → Users**.

## Where you land

Most people land on Home. See [Home](/help/home) for what it shows.

- If your home company has its own space (for example The Workshop Stoke), you land on that space instead.
- Client logins linked to a client company land on **My builds**.

## Finding your way round

You only see the parts of the Hub you have access to. Anything you can't use is hidden, not greyed out. If you need something you can't see, ask an admin.

### The old look

Home is a page of tiles grouped by area, such as **Me**, **Production Management**, **Finance** and **Operations**. Click a tile to open that area. Once you are in an area, a sidebar on the left lists its pages. Use **‹** to collapse it and **← Home** to go back. On a phone, tap the menu button in the top bar to open the sidebar.

### The new look

The new look has one sidebar with everything in it: **Home**, **My tasks** and **My productions** at the top, then groups for **Work**, **Money** and **Company**. Items with sub-pages open them underneath when you are in that area. Inside a production, the sidebar shrinks to icons and the production's own menu sits beside it.

On a phone there is a bar along the bottom with **Home**, **Search**, **Productions**, **Tasks** and **More**. **More** opens the full menu.

To try the new look, open the menu under your name in the top right and turn on **New look (beta)**. The page reloads. Turn it off the same way. See [Your account](/help/your-account).

## Searching

In the new look, press ⌘K on a Mac or Ctrl K on Windows to search the whole Hub. See [Search](/help/search).

## Getting help

- Click the question mark in the top bar to open the guides. Guides for the page you were on are listed first.
- If something is broken or you can't find an answer, raise it on the **Help desk**. In the new look it is under **Help and ideas**.

The top bar also has the bell for your notifications, and your name for **My Profile** and **Sign out**.
