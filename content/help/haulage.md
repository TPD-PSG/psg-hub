## Haulage on a production

Open the production and choose **Haulage**. Each row is one movement: a truck or van taking kit from one place to another.

### Adding a movement

1. Click **+ Add Movement**.
2. Enter the **Haulage Company**, **From** and **To**.
3. Set the **Departure** date and time, and the **Arrival** if you know it.
4. Add the **Driver Name** and **Vehicle Info**, for example "7.5t curtainside".
5. Describe the load in **Cargo / Description**, and add any **Notes**.
6. Enter the **Estimated Cost (£)**, and the **Actual Cost (£)** once you have it.
7. Pick a **Budget Line** if the cost belongs to one.
8. Tick **Rechargeable to client** if the client is paying for it.
9. Click **Add Movement**.

The list shows the company, route, departure and arrival, driver and vehicle, cargo, estimated and actual cost. Rechargeable movements are marked **Invoice**. Use **Edit** to change a movement, or **×** to delete it.

## How haulage affects the budget

- If a movement is linked to a budget line, its estimated cost counts towards that line's **PM Budget** and its actual cost towards its **Net Cost**.
- Movements not linked to a line get their own **Haulage (from movements)** row in the budget summary.
- Rechargeable movements are listed on the [Budget](/help/budget) in a rechargeables panel, with a total to invoice. They also show in [Finance](/help/finance) as pending invoices, and on the client budget report.

## The haulage page

Open **Haulage** from the main menu to see movements across every production you can see, in one list. Each row shows the production, so you can click through to it.

Search by company, location, cargo or production to narrow the list down. This is useful for checking which trucks are on the road this week, or every booking with one haulier.

## Exporting

On both pages, click **Export** and choose **Export PDF** or **Export Excel**. On the haulage page, the export follows your search.
