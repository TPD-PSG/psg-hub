## A production's H&S section

Open the production and choose **Health and safety** (**Health & Safety** in the old look). It has three parts: accidents and incidents, H&S documents, and the CDM toolbox talk.

## Accidents and incidents

Anyone who can open the show can click **Report an incident**. Choose the kind (accident, near miss, dangerous occurrence, ill health, or damage to kit or building), say when and where, and describe what happened. You can add up to four photos of the scene, but never of an injury. In an emergency, call 999 first.

Who sees reports:

- The show's PSG team staff see and follow up all its reports.
- **H&S leads** (admins, and staff an admin names) see every report, are told about each new one, and sign reports off.
- Everyone else sees only the reports they made.

### Crew without a login

Team staff click **QR code for crew**. Click **Download**, print it and put it up by the prompt desk, stage door and first-aid kit. Anyone can scan it and report without signing in. If the link gets out, click **Make a new code**; the old one stops working.

### Follow-up and RIDDOR

Open a report to add **Severity**, the investigation, **Root cause** and **Actions to stop it happening again**, each with who and a due date.

Under **RIDDOR**, pick whether it is reportable and which kind. The Hub sets the **Report to the HSE by** date: 10 days for most kinds, or 15 days when a worker is off for more than seven days. Add the date you reported and the **HSE reference**. The Hub does not decide for you, so check HSE guidance.

When follow-up is done, an H&S lead clicks **Sign off and close**.

## H&S documents

Drop risk assessments, method statements and other files into **General**. On a tour, each venue also has its own folder.

## CDM toolbox talk

Each show (or each tour venue) has a QR code. Crew scan it, or click **Open form**, to sign that they have been briefed. **Sign-off Records** lists each name, employer, position, date and what they agreed to. Click a date to filter, then **Export PDF**.

## The central Health and safety page

Open **Health and safety** from the main menu. It is for PSG staff. H&S leads see every show; other staff see their own shows.

It shows open reports and actions, **RIDDOR reports to make**, figures for the last 12 months, H&S documents on live shows with their review dates, and this week's toolbox talk sign-offs. Set a document's review date there. Admins choose the **H&S leads** at the bottom.

For the workshop, see [Workshop safety](/help/workshop-safety).
