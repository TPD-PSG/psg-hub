## Guides first

The **Guides** at /help explain how each part of the Hub works. Click the question mark in the top bar from any page and the guides for that page are shown first, under **For the page you were on**. You can also type in the search box, for example call sheet or freelancer, and click **Search**.

If the guides don't answer your question, or something isn't working, raise a ticket on the **Help Desk**. Both are under **Help and ideas** in the menu, and everyone can use them.

## Raising a ticket

1. Open the **Help Desk** and click **+ New Ticket**.
2. Write a short **Title** that sums up the problem or request.
3. In **Description**, say what you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead. Include the page address and any error message. You cannot attach a screenshot, so describe what you see on screen.
4. Pick a **Category**: Bug, Feature, Question or Other.
5. Pick a **Priority**: low, medium, high or urgent. Use urgent only when you cannot work.
6. Click **Submit Ticket**.

Your ticket appears in the **Open** column with a number, such as #42. Quote that number if you chase it.

For a wider idea that would help everyone, add it to the [Roadmap](/help/ideas-and-roadmap) too.

## Statuses

Tickets move through four columns:

- **Open**: received, not started.
- **In Progress**: someone is looking at it.
- **Resolved**: fixed or answered. The date is shown on the ticket.
- **Closed**: finished with.

Click a ticket to open it and see the full description.

## Who sees and answers tickets

Staff, freelancers and clients see only the tickets they raised. Admins handle the Help desk. They see every ticket by default, and can switch between **All tickets** and **My tickets**. Admins can see who raised each ticket, change its status and priority, and delete it.

The Hub does not send replies through the ticket. An admin will contact you directly, by email, message or phone, and then move the ticket on. Check the ticket's column to see where it has got to.
