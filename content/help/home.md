## What Home is for

Home shows what needs you, what is on today and where to go next. It only shows what you are allowed to see.

## Home in the old look

- **News**: a banner set by admins. Click the cross to hide it.
- Your company space banners, if you have any.
- **Waiting on you**: late tasks, tasks due in the next 7 days and urgent maintenance at Cockshute. Admins can click **All tasks** to see **Waiting on the team** instead.
- A search box for productions and your tasks. See [Search](/help/search).
- **What's going on today**: each show working today, and what it is doing.
- **Upcoming**: key dates in the next 60 days.
- **Quick links**: links to outside tools, set by admins in Hub Settings.
- **Companies**: one row per company, for PSG staff.
- Tiles for each area you can use, grouped by heading.

## Home in the new look

### Needs you

Things waiting for you, newest first. Click a line to go where it is done. It can include expenses and purchase invoices to approve (if you have Finance), your tasks due today or overdue, sales tasks due, over-budget lines on your shows, and show reports that didn't import (admins). If nothing needs you, the panel is not shown.

To clear a line you don't need to see, click the **×** at its end. This only hides it from your own Home: the expense isn't approved and the task isn't done. A hidden line comes back by itself if it changes, for example if a task gets a new due date. The panel shows how many you've hidden; click **Show them again** to bring them all back.

### Space cards

A card for Production Solutions Group with its live shows, and one for each company space you can see with its builds in the workshop.

### Your role panel

What this shows depends on your Home role. Admins set it for each person in **Admin → Users**. Admins default to director and everyone else to PM.

- **Director**: live shows, weighted pipeline (only with BD access), get-outs this week and the next key date.
- **PM**: your productions, each with its venue and next key moment.
- **Office**: expenses to approve, invoices to chase, Cockshute issues and open H&S reports.

Home never shows cash or bank figures.

### On today and Coming up

**On today** lists shows, get-ins and tour stops happening today. **Coming up** lists key dates and tour stops in the next 30 days. Click a line to open that production's schedule.

### Companies or My tasks

Directors see **Companies**, one row each with live shows, pipeline and dates in the next 14 days. Everyone else sees **My tasks**, with overdue tasks marked. See [Tasks](/help/tasks).

### Pinned shortcuts

**Pinned** holds up to six shortcuts to pages you use most. To change them:

1. Click **Edit** on the **Pinned** panel.
2. Tick up to six pages. Only pages you can open are listed.
3. Click **Save**.

**Back to the defaults** returns to the standard set for your Home role.
