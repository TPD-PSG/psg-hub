## Opening Hub Settings

Only admins can open Hub Settings. Go to **Admin**, then **Hub settings**.

## System Status

This shows when each background job last ran and whether it worked, such as the email import, backups and the daily notifications. Click **Refresh** to check again.

It also lists **Report emails not imported**: show report emails that the Hub couldn't import. These also appear in **Needs you** on Home for admins. **AI use, last 30 days** shows how often AI features were used.

## Entities and revenue targets

These are the group's companies. Deals and productions each belong to one, and the Director dashboard compares each company's confirmed and pipeline revenue with its target.

- Type a name and click **Add entity** to add a company.
- Type a target in a year's column. It saves when you click away.
- Use **↑** and **↓** to change the order, and **Rename** to change the name.
- **Archive** hides a company without losing anything. **Show archived** lists them, and **Restore** brings one back.
- **Delete** only works for a company nothing uses yet. Archive it otherwise.

### A company's space

A space gives a company its own home page in the Hub, in its own colours. People with that company as their home company land there when they sign in, and PSG staff can open it too.

Click **Space** on the company's row and fill in:

- **Switch the space on**: nobody can open it until this is ticked.
- **Web address**: the short name in its address, such as tws.
- **Kind of space**: **Workshop** (builds, planner, quotes and materials, like TWS) or **Rentals & Crew** (rental kit, packages, hires and availability; see [Rentals & Crew](/help/rentals-and-hires)).
- **Short name**, shown in the top bar.
- **Colour** and **Logo** (PNG, JPEG or WebP, under 1 MB).
- **Address** and **Phone**, used as the letterhead on its quotes.

Check the top bar preview, then click **Save space**. **Open the space →** takes you to it. A company with a space shows **Space ✓**.

Home companies are set in [Users and permissions](/help/users-and-permissions).

## News Banner

Type a message and click **Save banner**. It shows at the top of Home in the old look for everyone. Click **Clear** to remove it.

## Contact Sheet Sections

These are the default sections on every production's contact sheet, in order. Add one, move it with the arrows, or remove it. Removing a section doesn't touch existing contacts.

## Quick Links

Links to outside tools, shown on Home in the old look. Click **+ Add link**, give it an icon, a **Label** and a **URL**, and click **Add**. Click **Remove** to take one off.

## Seasonal Effects

Choose an **Effect**, such as Christmas snow, or **Auto** to follow the time of year, or **Off**. Set the **Intensity**. Click **Preview (8s)** to see it for eight seconds, then **Save settings**.
