## What it is for

The **Roadmap** is one shared list of ideas, plans and things to build in the Hub. Anyone with access can suggest an idea, and everyone can see where each one has got to.

Open it from **Help and ideas**, then **Ideas and roadmap**. You need the **Roadmap** module. Admins always have it.

The same list appears in each company space, such as The Workshop Stoke. An idea raised from inside a space carries a **From** badge with that company's name, so everyone can see where it came from.

## Suggesting an idea

1. Click **+ Add Idea**.
2. Give it a short title. Add a description if it helps: what you want, and why.
3. Pick a category: **General**, **Feature**, **Improvement**, **Bug** or **Infrastructure**.
4. Leave the status as **Ideas** unless you know otherwise.
5. Click **Add to Roadmap**.

Your name and the date are shown on the card.

If something is broken and stopping your work today, raise a ticket on the [Help desk](/help/help-desk) as well, so an admin sees it quickly.

## The columns

Items sit in five columns:

- **Ideas**: suggested, not yet agreed.
- **Planned**: agreed and waiting to be built.
- **In Progress**: being built now.
- **Done**: finished and in the Hub.
- **Won't Do**: decided against.

Items in **Done** and **Won't Do** are folded to one line. Click one to see its details.

Each column shows how many items it holds. Pinned items show at the top with a **Pinned** label.

## Moving and changing items

Hover over a card to see its buttons. The arrow buttons, such as **→ Planned**, move it to another column. **Edit** changes the title, description, category or status. **Delete** removes it after you confirm.

You can move, edit and delete the ideas you added. Admins can do this for every item, and can also **Pin** or **Unpin** an item to keep it at the top.

There is no voting. To support someone else's idea, tell an admin or add your reasons on the Help desk.
