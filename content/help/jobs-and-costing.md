## What a job is

A job is one piece of work we are doing, with a number such as J-2026-007. Its type is **Production management**, **Workshop build**, **Haulage (Night Run)** or **Other**. A job can be linked to a company, a client, the deal it came from and, for PM work, its production.

Jobs usually open themselves. Turning a won deal into a production opens its PM job, and **→ Job** on a won deal opens one for other work. See [BD pipeline](/help/bd-pipeline).

## Who sees what

Jobs are for PSG staff. Everyone can see jobs in the companies they have access to and log their own days.

Money is only shown to admins and staff with the **BD** or **Finance** module. They see prices, day rates, costs and margins, and only they can open a job by hand or change its status.

## Opening a job by hand

1. Go to **Jobs** and choose **+ Job**.
2. Enter a **Title**, then pick the **Type**, **Company** and **Client**.
3. If there will be no quote, enter an **Agreed price**.
4. Choose **Open job**.

## Logging days

On the job, under **Days logged**, pick the **Date**, the number of **Days** (in half days) and add a note. Choose **Log days**. Admins can log days for someone else using **Who**. You can remove your own entries.

Each entry is costed at the person's day rate, set by admins in **Admin**, then **Users**. Days logged by someone with no rate count as £0 and are flagged.

## Costing

The panel at the top of a job shows:

- **Price**: the accepted quote's price before VAT. If there is none, the agreed price, or for a PM job, the show's PM fee. Approved variations are added on.
- **Actual cost**: staff time, plus costs logged on the job, plus materials (delivered purchase orders and stock booked out). For a PM job it also includes items marked as a PSG expense on the show's budget.
- **Margin**: price minus actual cost, with the quoted cost and margin to compare against.

Money still committed on open purchase orders is shown separately and is not in the actual cost.

To add a cost, fill in **What for**, **Amount (£, ex VAT)**, date, supplier and category under **Costs**, then choose **Add cost**.

## The jobs list

Filter by **Active**, **Complete**, **Cancelled** or **All**. People who can see money also get a **Margin** table, grouped **By company**, **By client** or **By type**.

On a job, use **Mark complete**, **Cancel job** or **Reopen**. Links to its quotes and deal are at the bottom. See [Quotes](/help/quotes).
