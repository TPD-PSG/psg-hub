## When someone leaves

When a person leaves, their open work needs a new owner, and they should stop being able to sign in. The Hub does both in one step. Only admins can do this.

1. Open **Admin**, then **Users**.
2. On the leaver's row, click **Leaving…**.
3. The Hub checks what they have open and lists it, with a count for each kind.
4. Tick what to hand over.
5. Choose the person in **Hand over to**.
6. Leave **Also deactivate** ticked if they are going now. Untick it to hand over their work but leave their account on.
7. Click **Hand over**.

A message confirms how many items moved, and whether they were deactivated.

## What can be handed over

Only kinds where they have something open are listed. These can include:

- open tasks assigned to them
- tasks waiting for their sign-off
- show teams they are on (the new person is added; the leaver stays listed)
- upcoming crew bookings
- unfinished workshop build parts
- open BD opportunities they own
- clients they look after
- open help tickets assigned to them
- open H&S follow-up actions
- compliance register items they own
- their place as an H&S lead

Everything is ticked to start with, apart from upcoming crew bookings. Crew bookings are for a named person, so only tick them if the new person is really taking those dates.

Either everything you ticked moves, or nothing does. Finished work and their history stay under their name.

## Machine trainers

If the leaver is a named trainer on any workshop machines, the Hub lists those machines. Trainers are not moved, because a trainer has to be qualified on the machine. Add a qualified trainer to each one yourself.

## Who can take over

You can hand over to anyone with an active account, apart from clients.

## If they have nothing open

The dialog says so, and the button becomes **Deactivate** with their name. Click it to switch their account off.

## After they have gone

A deactivated person shows a **Deactivated** label in Users. Their button changes to **Hand over**, so you can move anything that turns up later. **Reactivate** lets them sign in again.

If you click **Delete** on someone with time logged on jobs, the Hub opens this handover instead, because deleting them would lose that time. See [Users and permissions](/help/users-and-permissions).
