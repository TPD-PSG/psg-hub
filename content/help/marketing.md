## What it is for

**Marketing** is where the team plans social media posts. Each post records what will go out, on which platforms, when, and for which production. The Hub does not publish posts itself. Use the **SocialPilot** button at the top to open SocialPilot in a new tab and schedule them there.

Open it from **Marketing** in the menu.

## Who can use it

People with the **Marketing** module can see, add and edit posts. Admins have it automatically. Only admins can delete a post.

If your account is limited to certain companies, posts linked to other companies' productions are hidden from you.

## Adding a post

1. Click **+ New Post**. On the calendar you can also hover over a day and click its **+** to start a post on that day at 9am.
2. Give it a title, such as Opening night announcement. This is the only box you must fill in.
3. Write the caption or copy.
4. Click each platform it is for: Instagram, Facebook, Twitter / X, LinkedIn or TikTok.
5. Set the status, the scheduled date and time, and the production if it is about a show.
6. Add a link, tags separated by commas, and notes if needed.
7. Click **Add Post**. When editing, the button is **Save Changes**.

## Statuses

Every post has one status:

- **Draft**: still being written.
- **Scheduled**: ready, with a date to go out.
- **Published**: has gone out.
- **Cancelled**: not going ahead.

Click a post to open its details. Click a status there to change it straight away. Click **Edit** to change anything else.

## Calendar and list

The page opens on **calendar**, showing one month. Use the arrows to move between months. Posts sit on the day they are scheduled, coloured by status and marked with their platforms. Only posts with a scheduled date appear on the calendar.

Click **list** to see every post in a table, including drafts with no date yet. The **Post list** link in the menu opens straight onto the list.

In either view, use the status buttons to show one status only, and click a platform to show only its posts. Click **All** to clear a filter.

## Not in this area yet

There are no approval steps, press ticket records or playing-week tags here. Agree approvals with the production team as usual and note the outcome in the post's notes.
