## What this page is for

**Materials & kit** holds the workshop's stock and purchase orders (POs). Any PSG staff member can use it, and sees stock and PO costs. Freelancers do not see it.

## Stock items

Click **+ Stock item** to add something the workshop keeps. Fill in:

- **Item**, such as 18mm ply
- **Section**: pick one such as Timber, Sheet materials or Paint & finishes, or type a new one
- **Unit**, **In stock now**, **Reorder at**, **£ each (ex VAT)**, **Usual supplier** and **Where it's kept**

Click **Add to stock**. The section buttons above the list show one section at a time.

## Reorder levels

When an item falls to its **Reorder at** level, it shows **Low · reorder** and appears in the yellow **Below reorder level** bar and on the space home page. Click **Order more** on an item, or its name in the yellow bar, to start a PO already filled in for it.

## Moving stock

Each item has three buttons:

- **Book out to a build**: takes the quantity out of stock and charges it to the build you pick
- **Return**: puts unused stock back, optionally from a build
- **Count**: sets the level to what you counted

**Recent changes** shows an item's history. You can also book out from a [build's page](/help/build-jobs).

## Raising a purchase order

1. Click **+ Raise PO**, or **Raise PO** on a build's page.
2. Pick a **Supplier**, or type a name if it is not in the list.
3. Under **For build**, pick the build, or leave it as **Workshop stock (no build)**.
4. Add lines. Link a line to a **Stock item** if it is going into stock.
5. Add any notes, then click **Submit** or **Save draft**.

A PO at or under the approval limit (£250 ex VAT unless an admin changes it) is approved straight away. Over the limit, the button says **Submit for approval**, and the PO waits for an admin or someone with Finance access. They click **Approve**, or **Send back** with a reason.

## Ordering and delivery

- **PDF for supplier** downloads the PO to send. Drafts and POs not yet approved are marked "not for ordering".
- **Mark ordered** once it is placed.
- **Mark delivered** when it arrives. Lines linked to stock items are added to stock.
- Admins and Finance can tick **Entered in Sage** on delivered POs.
- **Cancel PO** stops one that is not needed.

Filter the list with **Open**, **Awaiting approval**, **Delivered** or **All**.

Delivered POs and stock booked out to a build count towards that build's actual cost.
