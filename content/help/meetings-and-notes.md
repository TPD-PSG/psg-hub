## Meetings

Open the production and choose **Meetings**. Admins and PSG staff can add and edit meetings. Everyone who can open the show can read them.

### Adding a meeting

1. Click **+ New Meeting**.
2. Enter the **Title**, date and time, and **Location**.
3. Paste a **Recording link** from Zoom, Teams or OneDrive if there is one.
4. Under **Attendees**, tick people from the show's [contacts](/help/contacts). Tick a section heading to pick the whole section. Type anyone else in **Additional attendees**.
5. Write the **Agenda**.
6. Click **Create**.

### Transcripts and minutes

In the meeting form, under **GENERATE MINUTES FROM TRANSCRIPT**, there are three ways to get a transcript:

- Click **🎙 Record** and the Hub writes down what it hears as you speak. Click **⏹ Stop** when you are done. Recording stops if you leave the page.
- Click **📄 Upload transcript** to add a Zoom or Teams transcript (.vtt), an .srt file, or a Word transcript saved as .txt. Upload the transcript, not the recording itself.
- Paste the text into the box.

Then click **✨ Generate**. The Hub writes the minutes into the **Minutes** box. Check and edit them, then save. The transcript is saved with the meeting.

In the list, open **📋 Agenda**, **📝 Minutes** or **🗒 Transcript** to read each part. Click **▶ Watch the recording** to open the link, **↓ PDF** to download the meeting, **✏️** to edit it, or **🗑** to delete it.

### Minutes to tasks

Under a meeting with minutes, admins and PSG staff can click **Find action items**. The Hub suggests each action with an owner, due date and priority. Check them, edit any, and untick those you don't want. Then click **Create tasks** (the button shows how many). Nothing is created until you do. Actions already made into tasks are marked **Already a task from this meeting**.

## Tasks

Open **Tasks** for the show's task lists. Tasks sit in **General** unless you add a list with **+ List**. Tasks from meetings land here too, and open tasks show on the [Overview](/help/production-overview). See [Tasks](/help/tasks) for adding, assigning and signing off tasks.

## Notes

Open **Notes** for a board of quick notes about the show, such as decisions, reminders or things to check at the next venue.

Admins and PSG staff can click **+ Add Note**. Give it a **Title**, an optional body and a **Colour**, then click **Add**. Drag notes to put them in order. Click **✏️** on a note to edit it.
