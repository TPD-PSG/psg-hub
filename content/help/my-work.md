## Who My work is for

**My work** shows the build parts you are planned on, and the stages of a part that are yours on the [Workshop calendar](/help/workshop-calendar).

- Freelancers whose home company is TWS work from this page. Their menu only has **My work**, **Safety**, **Noticeboard** and **Fun Corner**.
- PSG staff can use it too, for the parts planned for them.

You only see the builds you have work on. There are no prices or costs on this page.

## Your parts by day

Your parts are grouped:

- **Today and on the go**
- **Next 7 days**
- **Later**
- **Done recently** (parts finished in the last 14 days)

Each part shows when it is, its hours, and "pencilled" if the dates are not confirmed yet. If you have nothing planned, the page says **Nothing planned for you yet**.

Click a part to open it. You will see the kind of work, the build's stage, and when it is due out.

## What you need for a part

An open part shows:

- **Stages**: each discipline stage on the part, its dates, who is on it and its status
- **Materials for this part**: what to use and how much
- **Machines** the part needs
- **Drawings and design**: the latest drawings, with a note if one is **Not signed off yet** or has **Changes asked for**, plus design files and model box photos

Check the drawings are signed off before you start building.

## Marking a part done

At the bottom of an open part, click **Mark** followed by the part's name, such as **Mark Flats and doors done**. If you ticked it by mistake, click **Not done after all**.

## Snags

**Snags on this build** lists the build's snags. Tick one when it is fixed. To add one, type it in and click **Add snag**.

## Photos

Under **Build photos**, click **+ Add photos**. On a phone this can open the camera. The workshop team see them on the build's page.

## Running late or asking to reschedule

On a stage that is yours:

- **Running late**: give the date you will now finish and a reason, then click **Report it**. The workshop managers are told straight away.
- **Ask to reschedule**: give a new start, how many days and a reason, then click **Send**. A workshop manager approves or declines it. If they approve, the stage moves.

While you wait, the stage shows "reschedule asked" with the dates you asked for. A late stage shows "running late" and the new finish date.
