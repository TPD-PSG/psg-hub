## The workshop noticeboard

**Noticeboard** holds notices, build photos and finished work. Anyone who can open the TWS space can post, including freelancers.

To post:

1. Choose what you are posting: **Notice**, **Photos** or **Showcase**.
2. Give it a **Title**. For a showcase, say **Where is it?**, for example on stage at the theatre.
3. For a notice, type the **Notice** itself.
4. Add up to 8 **Photos**. They are optional for a notice.
5. Pick the **Build** if it is about one.
6. Click **Post**.

PSG staff can **Pin** a post to keep it at the top, and **Unpin** it later. You can **Delete** your own posts. Admins can delete any post. Deleting a post deletes the photos posted with it, but build photos stay on the build.

**Share** on a photo on a [build's page](/help/build-jobs) also puts it here.

## Build of the month

**Build of the month** appears on the noticeboard and the space home page.

- Any TWS build with a photo added this month is up for it. Add photos on the build's page, on the noticeboard or from [Tablet mode](/help/tablet-mode).
- Everyone gets one vote a month. Click **Vote** under a build. Your choice shows **✓ Your vote**. You can change it until the month ends.
- When the month ends, the build with the most votes is shown as last month's winner.

## The TWS Fun Corner

**Fun Corner** is the workshop's way into the PSG Fun Corner. It needs the Fun Corner module, which an admin ticks in Admin → Users.

To send something in, fill in **Add something funny** with a **Title**, **The story**, and any **Photos or videos**, then click **Send it in**. It goes into the group's Fun Corner with the TWS badge.

Your stories show under **Waiting for the roundup** until they are revealed in the monthly roundup. You can delete your own while they wait. Past roundups are listed below. To see the whole group's Fun Corner, click **Open the PSG Fun Corner**.

## Roadmap

**Roadmap** is for PSG staff. It shows what is coming to the Hub, in columns: **Ideas**, **Planned**, **In progress**, **Done** and **Won't do**. It is the same list as the Hub roadmap, with each item tagged with where it was raised.

To suggest something, click **+ Add an idea**, give a **Title**, **Details**, **Type** and **Stage**, then click **Add to the roadmap**. Ideas added here are tagged to TWS.

Whoever raised an item, or an admin, can **Edit** it, use **Move to** to change its column, or **Delete** it. Only admins can **Pin** items.
