## What the planner shows

**Planner** puts the parts of every live build onto a person and a run of days. Each build has its own colour, listed at the top. Use **←** and **→** to move a fortnight at a time, and **This week** to come back.

PSG staff can plan. Freelancers do not see the planner. They see their own parts in [My work](/help/my-work).

## To plan

Parts with nobody or no date wait in the dashed **To plan** box. To add a part here, pick the **Build**, name the **Part**, set **Days** and **Hours**, and click **Add part**. Tick the pencil box to hold the dates while the build is still being quoted.

## Placing a part

- Drag a part onto a person's day in the grid, or
- click a part, then click a day in a person's row.

To unplan a part, drag it back into **To plan**.

When you click a part, buttons appear beside **To plan**:

- **− day** and **+ day** shorten or lengthen it
- **Pencil in** or **Confirm dates**
- **Back to To plan**, **Mark done** and **Delete**
- **Machines**, to tick the machines the part needs

Use **Add someone to the grid** to plan someone outside the TWS team. Finished parts are hidden.

## Pencilled parts

Parts added while a build is at the Quote stage are pencilled. They show hatched with a dashed edge. They become firm when the build's quote is accepted, or when you click **Confirm dates**.

## Clashes and warnings

Warnings appear above the grid:

- **Clash** (red): one person has two parts at once, or a part while they are booked on a show elsewhere in the Hub. Show bookings appear in their own lane.
- **Possible clash (pencilled)** (amber): a clash that involves a pencilled part.
- **⚠ Not inducted**: the person is not inducted on a machine the part needs.
- **⚠ Kit not usable**: a machine the part needs is out of use or overdue a check.

These are warnings only. They do not stop you planning. Follow the links to fix them on the [Safety](/help/workshop-safety) page.

## Builds and capacity

Below the grid, **Builds & capacity** shows each build as a bar from its start to its due-out date. Choose **8 weeks**, **3 months**, **6 months** or **1 year**, and scroll sideways to see further ahead. Builds still at Quote show as pencilled.

The **Crew-days booked** row shows, for each week, the crew-days booked (confirmed, then pencilled) against what the team has: team size times five days. A week over capacity is outlined in red with a **!**. Crew-days count Monday to Friday.
