## Opening the overview

In the new look, a production opens on **Overview**. You can also pick it from the **Plan** group in the production's menu. The old look does not have this page; it opens on Schedule.

The overview only shows things from sections you can see. If a section is hidden for you, its tile says **Not in your sections** and its panel is left out. Most panels have a link in the corner to open the full section.

## The four tiles

- **Now:** for a tour, the venue the show is at today and which stop it is (for example **Stop 3 of 8**), or **Not on** between stops. For a single-venue show, the show's venue.
- **Next:** on a tour, the next venue and its get-in date. Click either tile to open [Tour stops](/help/tour-stops-and-advancing).
- **Budget:** the total allocation and the percentage spent, measured on net cost. If any lines are over, it says how many and turns red. Click to open the [Budget](/help/budget).
- **Show reports:** how many reports came in over the last seven days, with the number of faults and absences. Click to open [Show reports](/help/show-reports).

## This week

A list of the next seven days: schedule events with their time and location, and tour stop get-ins or openings. Click **Schedule** to see the full [schedule](/help/schedule-and-key-dates).

## Advancing

For a tour, this shows the next venue and how far its advancing has got, from **Triage** to **Closed / pre contra**. The current step is marked **Now**. For a single-venue show it shows the show's own venue. When a tour has no more stops, it says so.

## Crew, tasks and the latest report

- **Crew on this week:** crew booked on the show whose dates fall in the next seven days, with their role. The first six are listed.
- **Open tasks:** the number of open tasks and the first four by due date, with who has each one. Click a task to open it in [Tasks](/help/tasks).
- **Latest show report:** the date, performance and venue of the most recent report, marked **Clean** or with how many sections were flagged.

The overview loads fresh each time you open it. If it cannot load, use the menu to open a section directly.
