## The productions list

Open **Productions** to see every active production you can see. The tiles at the top count **Active** productions, those **In Production**, **My Productions** (ones you are on the team for) and those **Opening This Month**.

Use **All Active** and **My Productions** to switch the list. Each row shows the logo or colour, type, venue, dates, the team and three badges: **PENCIL** or **CONFIRMED**, the announcement status, and the production status.

Admins and staff with access to all productions see every show. Clients, freelancers and other staff only see the productions they are on the team for.

## Adding a production

PSG staff can click **+ New Production**.

1. Enter the **Production Name** and pick a **Type**.
2. Set the **Status** (Planning, In Production or Closed) and **Announcement** (NDA, Not Announced, On Sale or Open).
3. Add the **Venue**, **Start Date**, **End Date** and any **Notes**.
4. Click **Create Production**. The new production opens.

## The production header

The top of each production shows its name, type, statuses, client, venue and dates.

The round initials are the team. Admins and staff click **+** to add someone, or click a person to remove them. Clients and freelancers only see a show once they are on its team.

Admins also see **Edit**, **Archive** and **Delete**. **Edit** opens the full details: internal status (**Pencil** or **Confirmed**), production manager, company, client, venue from the venue directory, **This is a touring production**, calendar colour, show report email, SharePoint folder and logo. If a SharePoint folder is set, clicking the logo opens it.

## Archiving

Click **Archive** to move a finished show to **Archived Productions**. It becomes read-only. Find it from **View archived productions** at the bottom of the list, and click **Restore** to bring it back.

**Delete** removes the production and all its data for good.

## The sections menu

Each production is split into sections.

- **Old look:** the sidebar lists the sections. A production opens on **Schedule**.
- **New look:** the menu groups them under **Plan** (Overview, Schedule, Tour stops, Advancing, Call sheets), **People** (Crew, Contacts, Accommodation), **Money** (Budget), **Logistics** (Haulage), **Records** (Documents, Links, Health and safety, Show reports) and **Work** (Tasks, Notes, Meetings). A production opens on [Overview](/help/production-overview). On a phone, pick a section from the list under the top bar.

**Tour stops** only shows for touring productions. By default, clients and freelancers do not see **Budget**, **Crew**, **Advancing** or **Links**. An admin can change this in [Users and permissions](/help/users-and-permissions).
