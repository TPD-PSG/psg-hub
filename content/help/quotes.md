## Starting a quote

Quotes are for PSG staff with the **BD** module. Choose **+ New quote**, add a title and type of work, and under **Start from** pick a template or **Blank quote**. **+ Quote** on a deal copies its client and company.

## The header

Set the **Client**, **BD deal**, **Company (on the PDF)**, **Type of work**, **Valid until** and **VAT %**. **Customer PO**, **Payment terms** (such as **In advance**), **Due date**, **Shipping method** and **Ship to** fill the boxes on a company's sales quote layout. With no due date, the build's due-out date is used.

## Items and lines

An item groups lines under a subtotal. Choose **+ New item**, name it, then **Start item**. Use **＋ line** on an item, or **＋** on a line to add below it.

In the **Add a line** bar, type a description to search labour rates, stock and what we last paid on purchase orders. Pick a result or **Custom line: keep what I typed**. Leave the price blank and it is set to cost plus the **Markup on cost** figure. Press Enter to add.

- Each line is **Material**, **Labour** or **Misc**.
- **Hide line** keeps a line off the PDF but in the totals. **No £** shows it without a price.
- **Leave out** takes a whole item out of the totals and the PDF.
- Type a figure in an item's price box to set its price. A hidden **Item price adjustment** line makes up the difference.
- Drag a line by its handle to move it, even into another item.
- Use the arrow beside an item, or **Collapse all**, to fold items away.

**Our cost**, margin and **Internal notes** never appear on the PDF. **Save as template** lets you reuse a quote.

## The PDF

Under **On the client's PDF**, choose **One price per item** or **Full breakdown**, with optional section subtotals. **Preview** shows it on screen; **PDF for client** downloads it. A quote for a company with its own Hub space uses that company's **Sales Quote** layout. Otherwise it uses the PSG layout.

## Statuses

Quotes go from **Draft** to **Sent** (**Mark sent**), then **Accepted** or **Declined**. **Reopen** takes it back to Sent. Only admins can delete a quote that is not a draft.

Accepting a quote makes its price the job's price and locks its lines and VAT. Later changes go through a variation on the job.

## Quotes and builds

On a workshop quote, choose **Start a build from this quote**. Each item becomes a part on the planner, pencilled until the quote is accepted. Labour lines set its time; material lines, its materials. See [Build jobs](/help/build-jobs) and [Jobs and costing](/help/jobs-and-costing).
