## What Sales is for

The Sales area tracks the product brands we are working with or hoping to work with, such as lighting or rigging manufacturers, and the jobs that need doing for each one. It is separate from the [BD pipeline](/help/bd-pipeline), which is for client deals.

Sales is for admins and staff with the **Sales** module. You find it in the menu under **BD pipeline**, as **Brands** and **Sales tasks**.

## Adding a brand

1. Open the **Brands** tab and choose **+ Add Brand**.
2. Enter the **Brand name**.
3. Pick a **Category** (lighting, sound, cable, staging, rigging, video, consumables or other), a **Stage** and a **Priority**.
4. Add the **Website**, the **Rep name**, **Rep email** and **Rep phone**, and any **Notes**.
5. Choose **Save**.

## Stages

A brand moves through **Prospect**, **Contacted**, **Samples**, **Negotiating** and **Active**. It can also be **Paused** or **Lost**.

Use the stage buttons above the list to show only brands at one stage. Click again to show them all.

## Working with a brand

Click a brand to open it in the list. You see its contact details, notes and open tasks.

- Click a stage button along the bottom to move the brand to that stage.
- Choose **+ Add** under **Tasks** to add a task for this brand.
- Choose **Edit** to change its details, or **Delete** to remove it.

A dot and number on the brand's row show how many open tasks it has.

## Sales tasks

Open the **Tasks** tab to see every open sales task, with its brand, priority and due date. Overdue dates show in red.

1. Choose **+ Add Task**.
2. Say what needs doing. Link it to a **Brand** if it belongs to one, and set a **Priority**, **Due date** and notes.
3. Choose **Save**.

Tick the box beside a task when it is done. Tick **Show completed** to see finished tasks again. Use **Edit** to change a task, or the cross to delete it.

Sales tasks that are due today or overdue can also appear on your Home page.
