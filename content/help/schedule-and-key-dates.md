## What is on Schedule

Open the production and choose **Schedule**. It has three parts: **Key Dates**, the **Performance Schedule** (single-venue shows only) and the **Daily Schedule**.

## Key dates

Key dates are the big milestones, such as first rehearsal, fit up, press night and get out. They show on the central [Calendar](/help/calendar-and-schedule).

1. Click **+ Add Key Date**.
2. Pick a **Type**, for example **Load In**, **Technical Rehearsal**, **Preview** or **Get Out**.
3. Add a **Custom label** if you want different wording, such as "Press Night, Lyttelton".
4. Set the **Start date**, and an **End date** if it runs over several days.
5. Add any **Notes** and click **Add**.

Use **Edit** or **Delete** on a key date to change or remove it.

## Performance schedule

For shows that are not touring, you can set which days have shows.

1. In the grid, click **M** for a matinee and **E** for an evening on each day of the week. A day can have either or both.
2. Click **Save pattern**.

The calendar below covers the run, from the first preview, press night or performance key date to the get out (or the production's end date). Click a day to switch its evening show on or off. Right-click to switch its matinee. Days you change by hand get an orange dot. The count at the top shows the total evenings and matinees.

If no calendar shows, add an opening and closing key date first.

## Daily schedule

The daily schedule is the list of timed events: rehearsals, tech, meetings and performances.

1. Click **+ Add Event**.
2. Enter a **Title** and pick a **Type** (Rehearsal, Tech, Dress Rehearsal, Performance, Meeting, Workshop or Other).
3. Set the **Start** and **End** date and time.
4. Add a **Location** and **Notes** if needed, then click **Save**.

Events are listed in time order with a coloured type badge. Click **Delete** on an event to remove it.

Events also appear on that day's [call sheet](/help/call-sheets) and in **This week** on the [Overview](/help/production-overview).

## Exporting

Click **Export** beside **+ Add Event** and choose **Export PDF** or **Export Excel** to download the daily schedule.
