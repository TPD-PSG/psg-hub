## Searching in the new look

You can open search from anywhere in the new look:

- press ⌘K on a Mac or Ctrl K on Windows
- click the search box in the top strip
- click **Search the Hub** on Home
- on a phone, tap **Search** in the bottom bar

Type at least two letters. Results appear as you type, grouped by type. Use the up and down arrow keys and press Enter to open one, or click it. Press Esc to close search.

Before you type, search shows the last few things you opened from it under **Recent**.

## What you can find

- **Pages**: any page in the menu, such as "expenses" for Finance › Expenses.
- **Productions**: by name or venue, including archived ones.
- **Venues** and **Hotels**: by name or city.
- **People**: crew by name, and for admins, PSG staff accounts.
- **Suppliers**: by name.
- **Clients** and **Deals**: from the BD pipeline.
- **Tasks**: by title.
- **Guides**: these help guides.
- **Invoices**: by reference, party or description.

## What you will see

Search only shows what you could open anyway.

- Productions are limited to the shows you can see. If you have team only access, that means shows you are on. If you can only see some companies, it means their shows.
- Venues, hotels and productions need the Productions or Venues areas.
- Crew only show for PSG staff with access to Productions.
- Clients and deals need Business Dev. Deals are also limited to your companies.
- Invoices need Finance.
- Tasks are the ones assigned to you or that you created. PSG staff who can see all productions also find tasks on productions.
- Suppliers need the Suppliers area.
- Guides are open to everyone.

If something is missing that you think you should see, ask an admin to check your access. See [Users and permissions](/help/users-and-permissions).

## Searching in the old look

The old look has a search box on Home. It matches production names and the titles of your tasks. Click a result to open it.

## Searching the guides

The guides page has its own search box. Type a word such as "call sheet" or "freelancer" to find guides about it. If nothing matches, ask on the [Help desk](/helpdesk).
