## Where show reports come from

Open the production and choose **Show reports** (**Show Reports** in the old look). Reports arrive in three ways.

### From email

Stage management email their PDF report to the reports inbox, reports@production-solutions.group. The Hub checks the inbox regularly, reads each PDF and files it under the production whose name is in the email subject. Put the show's name in the subject line so it lands in the right place.

To check now, click **Import from Email**. Choose **New only** for unread reports, or **All emails** to go back through older ones as well. A status line shows progress.

A report the Hub could not match to a show is marked **Unmatched**.

### From a PDF

Drop PDF show reports onto the box that says **Drop PDF show reports here, or click to select**. You can drop several at once. Each one is read and added.

### By hand

Click **+ Add Report** and fill in the **Date**, **Performance** (for example "Evening, 7:30pm"), **Venue**, **Attendance** and **Notes**.

## Reading a report

Reports are listed with the latest performance first. Click one to open it. Each department's notes sit in their own box, and the timings are highlighted. Where there is an original PDF, click **PDF** to download it.

Use **Edit** to correct a report, or **Delete** to remove it. If some imports came in with empty fields, click **Re-parse Empty** to read those PDFs again.

## Flagging problems

Click the flag beside a section heading, such as Sound or Wardrobe, to mark a problem that needs following up. Flagged sections are highlighted, and the flags show on the report in the list. Click the flag again to clear it.

## Gaps and repeats

The Hub checks the performance numbers. If a preview or performance has no report, or more than one, a warning lists which numbers to check.

## The weekly digest

At the top of the section, the digest sums up one week: the performances, house figures, running times, which departments flagged problems, how many incidents and absences there were, and any **Repeat faults** (the same cue or kit at two or more shows). Use **← Week** and **Week →** to move between weeks. Click a report in the digest to open it.

Every Monday, PSG staff on the show's team get a Hub notification with last week's digest. Click it to open that week.

The latest report and this week's counts also show on the [Overview](/help/production-overview).
