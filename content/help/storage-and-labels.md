## What set storage is for

**Storage** is a register of built pieces and where they have been put. Nothing is charged for it. Every move is logged. PSG staff can change storage. Freelancers do not see the Storage page.

## Locations

The **Locations** card lists the places pieces go, such as bays, racking, containers or offsite units, with how many pieces are in each.

- To add one, type a name such as "Bay 3" and click **Add**.
- Click **Manage** to **Rename** a location, or **Retire** one that is no longer used. A location must be empty to retire it. **Restore** brings it back.

Click a location to show only its pieces. **Not placed** shows pieces that are in store but not yet in a location.

## Putting pieces into store

1. Click **+ Put pieces into store**.
2. Type the pieces, one per line. Add "x2" for more than one, for example "Palace flats x2".
3. Pick **From build** and **Where it's been put**, and add any notes.
4. Click **Put in store**.

You can do the same from the storage card on a build's page, which only shows that build's pieces. See [Build jobs](/help/build-jobs).

## Moving pieces

Each piece has these buttons:

- **Move** (or **Place** if it has no location yet): pick the new location
- **Gone out**: say where it went, such as delivered or scrapped, then click **Mark gone out**
- **Back in store**: when a piece returns
- **Size & weight**: name, how many, size in mm, weight in kg each, and notes
- **History**: every move, who made it and when
- **Delete**: only for a piece added by mistake. Use **Gone out** when a piece leaves.

Search by piece, build, client or location. Use **In store**, **Gone out** or **All** to filter.

## Printing QR labels

Click **Print QR labels** on the Storage page to label the pieces shown, or on a build's storage card to label that build's pieces. There is one label per item, so a piece ×6 gives six labels marked "1 of 6" and so on, 8 to an A4 sheet. Each label has the QR code, piece name, build, client and size. Click **Print labels** to print them.

## Scanning a label

Scanning a QR label opens the piece's page. Anyone who can open the TWS space can see it. It shows:

- **Where it is** now, its size and notes
- **History** of its moves
- the build's **Drawings** with their sign-off status
- the build's **FR certificates**, and any **Build photos**

Staff see a link to move it or mark it gone out in Storage.
