## What it is for

The **Supplier Register** is one list of the companies PSG works with, with a contact name, phone, email, website and address for each. Use it to find the right number quickly instead of searching old emails.

Open it from **Operations**, then **Supplier register**.

## Who can see and change it

- People with the **Suppliers** module can see the register. Freelancers can always see it.
- Only admins can add, edit or delete suppliers. If a supplier is missing or out of date, ask an admin or raise it on the [Help desk](/help/help-desk).

## Finding a supplier

The top of the page shows how many suppliers are on record.

- Type in **Search suppliers** to match on the supplier's name, the contact's name or the email address.
- Click a category to show only those suppliers: **Venues**, **Contractors**, **Equipment**, **Transport**, **Catering**, **Marketing**, **Legal**, **Insurance** or **Other**. Click **All** to show everyone again.

Search and category work together. If nothing matches, the page says so.

On each card, click the phone number to call it, the email to start an email, or the website to open it in a new tab.

## Adding a supplier (admins)

1. Click **+ Add Supplier**. The **New Supplier** form opens at the top of the page.
2. Enter the name. This is the only box you must fill in.
3. Pick a category. If none fits, use **Other**.
4. Add the contact name, email, phone, website, address and any notes that would help a colleague, such as account numbers or opening hours.
5. Click **Add supplier**.

Suppliers are listed in name order.

## Editing or removing a supplier (admins)

Click **Edit** on the card, change the details and click **Save changes**. Click **Cancel** to leave without saving.

Click **Del** to remove a supplier. You are asked to confirm, and it cannot be undone.

## Related guides

- Venues have their own, fuller records in the Venues area. Add a venue here only if you want it listed alongside other suppliers.
- Contractors who work on the estate can also be kept as contacts on [Cockshute Industrial Estate](/help/cockshute-estate).
