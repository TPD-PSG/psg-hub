## What tablet mode is for

**Tablet mode** turns a tablet in the workshop into a shared station. Anyone in the TWS team can walk up, tap their name and record their work without logging in themselves. The buttons are big and there are few words.

A PSG staff member opens **Tablet mode** from the TWS menu and leaves the tablet on that page. The side menu is hidden while it is open. To get out, tap **Leave tablet mode** at the top.

## Tap your name

The first screen asks **Who are you?** and lists the team, with what each person is clocked on to, or **Not clocked on**.

Tap your name to see your options. If you tapped the wrong name, tap **← Not me / back**. After each action, or after 90 seconds with nothing done, the tablet goes back to the list of names, ready for the next person.

## Clocking on and off

- **Clock on**: pick the build you are working on. Builds you are planned on today come first, with what is planned.
- **Switch build**: moves you to another build. This clocks you off the last one.
- **Clock off**: at the end of the job or the day.

When you clock off or switch, the time is logged as days on that build. It is rounded to the nearest half day, with at least half a day logged. The clock times go in the note. Anything under 15 minutes is treated as a mistake and not logged. The days show under **Days logged** on the [build's page](/help/build-jobs).

## Warnings

When you pick a build, the tablet may show a warning:

- **⚠ Not inducted on**: you are planned on a machine you have not been inducted on. Do not use it until a trainer has inducted you.
- **⚠ Don't use**: a machine the work needs is out of use or overdue a check.

These are warnings, not blocks. You can still clock on. See [Workshop safety](/help/workshop-safety).

## Taking a build photo

1. Tap your name, then **Take a build photo**.
2. If you are clocked on, it uses that build. Otherwise pick the build, or tap **Different build**.
3. Tap **Open the camera** and take the photo.

The photo goes into the build's photos, captioned with your name and "from the tablet".

## Adding a snag

1. Tap your name, then **Add a snag**.
2. Pick the build if you are not clocked on to it.
3. Type **What needs sorting?** and tap **Add snag**.

The snag appears under **Snags & tasks** on the build's page, with your name.
