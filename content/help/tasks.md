## My tasks

Open **My tasks** in the new look, or the **My Tasks** tile on Home in the old look. The page lists tasks assigned to you, tasks you created and tasks waiting for your sign-off. They are grouped by production, with **Company-wide** tasks last.

Use the buttons at the top to filter: **Active**, **Sign-off**, **Done** or **All**.

You need the Tasks area to use this page. Freelancers always have it.

## Adding a task

1. Click **+ Add Task**. To add one straight to a production, click **+ add** beside that production's heading.
2. Enter a **Title**. Add a **Description** if it helps.
3. Set the **Status**, **Priority** and **Due date**.
4. Choose someone in **Assign to**, or type a name in **Or name (free text)** for someone outside the Hub.
5. Click **Add Task**.

The person you assign gets a notification. Tasks with a due date also show on the [Calendar](/help/calendar-and-schedule). Once a day, people get a reminder for tasks due that day or overdue.

## Updating a task

Click a task to open it. Change anything, then click **Save**. Click **Delete** to remove it.

Tick the box beside a task to mark it done. Tick it again to reopen it.

## Sign-off

Some tasks need someone else to approve them before they count as done.

1. Open the task and tick **Requires sign-off**.
2. Choose who in **Sign-off from**, then save.

When the task is ticked, it goes to **Awaiting Sign-off** instead of **Done**, and the sign-off person is notified. They see it at the top of their page under **Awaiting your sign-off**, and click **Approve** to finish it.

## Task lists on a production

On a production, open **Tasks**. Tasks sit in **General** unless you put them in a list.

- Click **+ List**, type a name and click **Add**.
- Click a list to see its tasks. **+ Add Task** then adds to that list.
- Hover over a list to rename or delete it. Deleting a list does not delete its tasks; they go back to General.
- To move a task, open it and choose **Move to list**.

## Tasks from meetings

On a production's **Meetings** section, PSG staff can turn the minutes into tasks.

1. Open the meeting and click **Find action items**. The Hub reads the minutes and suggests each action with an owner, due date and priority.
2. Check each one, edit it, and untick any you don't want.
3. Click **Create tasks** (the button shows how many) to make them. Nothing is created until you do.

Tasks made this way show **From:** and the meeting's name. Click it to go back to the meeting.
