## Tour stops

**Tour stops** only appears for touring productions. An admin turns this on by ticking **This is a touring production** in the production's **Edit** form.

### Adding a stop

1. Open the production and choose **Tour stops** (**Tour Stops** in the old look).
2. Click **+ Add Stop**.
3. Type in **Venue** to search the [venue directory](/help/venues) and pick one. If it is not there, add it on the Venues page first.
4. Set the **Load-in date**, **Get-out date**, **First performance** and **Last performance**.
5. Add any **Notes**, such as special requirements or local contacts, and click **Add Stop**.

Stops are numbered in order. Each shows the venue, city, capacity and dates. Stops the tour has already left move to the bottom under **Finished** and are faded.

### Working with a stop

- **Notes** opens the stop's notes. Type and click away to save. A dot means the stop has notes.
- **↓ PDF** on a stop downloads a venue sheet for that stop.
- **Edit** changes the venue or dates. **Remove** deletes the stop.

Use **↓ PDF** or **↓ XLSX** at the top to download the whole tour schedule.

Tour stops feed the [Overview](/help/production-overview), the venue on each day's [call sheet](/help/call-sheets), and the per-venue folders in [Health and safety](/help/health-and-safety).

## Advancing

Open **Advancing** to track how far each venue has got. The board has seven columns:

1. **Triage**
2. **Rider Released**
3. **Contra Estimate In**
4. **Plans Drawn**
5. **Full Email Released**
6. **In Venue**
7. **Closed / Pre Contra**

On a tour, each stop has a card with its venue and dates. Drag a card to the next column as the work moves on. A single-venue show has one card for its venue, which you drag in the same way.

By default, clients and freelancers do not see Advancing.

## Contra

Each card has a **Contra** link. Click it to show two boxes: **Est.** for the estimate and **Act.** (or **Actual**) for the actual figure. Type an amount and click away, or press Enter, to save. The card then shows the amounts.

Contra figures also appear in the **Venue Contra** table on the [Budget](/help/budget), with a total for the tour.
