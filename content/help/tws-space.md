## What the space is

The Workshop Stoke (TWS) has its own space in the PSG Hub, with its own logo, colours and menu. It uses the same login and data as the rest of the Hub.

The menu down the left lists the space's own pages: **Home**, **My work**, **Build board**, **Build jobs**, **Planner**, **Workshop calendar**, **Quotes**, **Materials & kit**, **Storage**, **Aftercare**, **Safety**, **Tablet mode**, **Noticeboard**, **Fun Corner** and **Roadmap**. On a phone, tap the three lines at the top left. **Shared with PSG** links to **My tasks**, **Calendar** and **Help desk**.

## Who sees what

- PSG staff (admins and members) see the full menu.
- Freelancers whose home company is TWS only see **My work**, **Safety**, **Noticeboard** and **Fun Corner**. If a freelancer opens any other page they are taken to [My work](/help/my-work).
- Clients never see the space. They use [My builds](/help/client-logins).

## The home page

**Home** shows:

- A yellow **things need you** bar: builds past their due-out date, builds going out within a week that have not reached Finish, stock below its reorder level, purchase orders waiting for approval and parts still to plan. Click a line to go straight to it.
- **Live jobs**: a card per build with its stage, client, a progress bar and when it goes out.
- **Collections due**: what is leaving in the next four weeks, and whether we deliver or the client collects.
- **Build schedule · this week**: a Monday to Sunday strip for each build.
- **Materials & kit**, noticeboard posts, build of the month and the team. There are no prices here.

## The build board

**Build board** shows every live build in a column for each stage: Quote, Confirmation, Technical Design, Build, Finish and Delivery. Use the arrow buttons on a card to move a build back a stage or on to the next one. A build cannot move to Build, Finish or Delivery until its drawings are signed off. See [Build jobs](/help/build-jobs).

## Build jobs list

**Build jobs** lists every TWS build, sorted by due-out date. Use **Live**, **Complete**, **Cancelled** or **All** to filter. If you can see money, you also see price, spend and margin.

## Starting a new build

1. Click **+ New build job** on the home page or the build jobs list.
2. Fill in **Build**, and if you know them, **Client**, **For production**, **Build starts**, **Due out of the workshop** and **Leaving by**.
3. Click **Create build**.

The build opens at the Quote stage. Won deals for TWS open their own build at Confirmation.

## Getting back to the PSG Hub

Click **PSG Hub** in the top bar, or **PSG Hub home** at the bottom of the menu.
