## Opening Users

Only admins can manage users. Open **Admin**, then **Users**, or click **Manage Users** in the menu under your name.

## Adding someone

- **Invite User** sends them an email with a link to set their own password. The link lasts 7 days.
- **+ Add User** creates the account straight away with a password you choose.

Fill in their **Name**, **Email**, **Role** and access, then click **Send Invitation** or **Create User**.

People who have been invited but haven't set up their account are listed under **Invited**. Click **Resend** to send a fresh link, or **Cancel invite** to stop the old one working.

## Roles

- **Admin (full access)**: sees and does everything.
- **Member**: PSG staff. Sees the areas you tick.
- **Freelancer**: always gets Productions, Venues, Tasks, Suppliers, Fun Corner and Ask me anything, whatever is ticked. Only sees productions they are on.
- **Client**: always gets Productions and Venues only, and only the productions they are on. A client can be linked to a **Client company** so they see its builds in **My builds** and sign off its drawings.

## Access settings

These show for everyone except admins.

- **Module Access**: tick the areas they can use, such as Business Dev, Productions, Tasks, Finance and Marketing.
- **Visible Sections**: untick production sections to hide them, such as Budget or Crew. Clients and freelancers have Budget, Crew, Advancing and Links hidden to start with.
- **Production Access**: **All productions**, or **Team only** with a list to tick the shows they are on.
- **Companies they can see**: **All companies**, or **Only these** with the companies ticked. This limits productions, BD, quotes, jobs and company spaces. Records with no company set are hidden from them.

## Other settings

- **Home company**: if that company has its own space, they land there when they sign in and the top bar takes its colours.
- **Schedule colour**: their colour on the calendar's staff bookings.
- **Cost per day**: for staff, what a day of their time costs PSG on job costing.

To reset a password, click **Edit** and fill in **New password**.

## New look and Home role

Click a row's **New look** button to turn the new look on or off for that person.

For admins and members, the **Home** menu sets which panels the new look's Home shows them: **Director**, **PM** or **Office**. Admins default to Director and members to PM. See [Home](/help/home).

## Deleting and deactivating

**Delete** removes someone for good. If they have time logged on jobs, the Hub offers to hand over their work and deactivate them instead. Deactivated people can't sign in, but their history stays. **Reactivate** lets them back in.

When someone leaves, use **Leaving…** first. See [Leavers and handover](/help/leavers-and-handover).
