## Reading the calendar

**Workshop calendar** shows every live build as a timeline. Each build opens out into its pieces (the build's parts), and each piece into its stages, one per discipline. A faint bar shows the build's dates and a red ◆ marks its due-out day.

Click the arrow beside a build to fold it away. Filters at the top:

- **Weeks**: how far ahead to show, from 4 to 26
- **Discipline**, **Build** and **Person**
- **Hide done**

The calendar is for PSG staff. Freelancers see their own stages in [My work](/help/my-work).

## Disciplines

Disciplines are the stages a piece can go through. The workshop starts with Pre-production, Technical drawing, CNC, Carpentry, Fabrication and Scenic. To change the list, click **Change the list** under **Disciplines**. You can rename one, add a new one such as Upholstery, or click **Stop using**.

## Where stages come from

When a quote is linked to a build, each of its sections becomes a piece. A labour line named after a discipline, such as "Carpentry" or "CNC cutting", becomes that stage on the piece, with its hours. See [TWS quotes](/help/tws-quotes).

You can also add a stage by hand. Click **+ stage** on the piece's row and pick a discipline.

## Moving and changing stages

- Drag a bar to move it to other days.
- Drag its right edge to make it longer or shorter.
- A stage with no dates says **click a day**. Click a day on its row to start it there.
- Click a bar to open its editor. There you can change **Discipline**, **Starts**, **Days**, who is doing it and **Status** (Not started, On it or Done).

**Take off the calendar** clears the dates. **Remove stage** deletes it.

## Running late and reschedule requests

In a stage's editor:

- **Running late**: give the new finish date and a reason, then click **Report it**. The bar shows **late**.
- **Ask to reschedule**: give a new start, days and a reason, then click **Send request**. The bar shows **⇄** and the proposed dates.

The person doing a stage can also do this from My work. Requests and late stages are listed at the top of the calendar. A workshop manager clicks **Approve and move it** or **Decline**. Approving moves the stage.

## Workshop managers

Workshop managers hear when something is running late and decide reschedule requests. Admins tick who they are under **Workshop managers**. If nobody is ticked, every admin does it.

## References per build

Click **Refs** beside a build to see its drawings with their sign-off status, design files, model box photos and links. To add more, use **Add references on the build**. See [Build jobs](/help/build-jobs).
