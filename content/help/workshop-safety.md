## The Safety page

**Safety** is open to staff and freelancers. It covers incidents, equipment checks and machine inductions. In an emergency, call 999 first.

## Accidents and incidents

Anyone who can open the space can report. Click **Report an incident** and choose what happened: an accident, near miss, dangerous occurrence, ill health, or damage to kit or the building.

The workshop's own staff and the H&S leads see every report. Everyone else sees only the reports they filed.

### The QR code for the floor

People without a login can report from their phone. Staff click **QR code for the floor**, then **Download** it to print and put up by the machines, first-aid kit and loading door. If the code gets out, click **Make a new code**. Old copies then stop working.

## Equipment and checks

Every item of kit is listed with its checks and when each is next due. Everyone in the space can see it. Workshop staff add kit and record results.

To add kit, click **+ Add equipment**, say what it is and pick the **Kind of kit**. The usual checks are filled in, for example a PAT test for power tools, a LOLER thorough examination every 6 months for lifting gear, and a COSHH review for substances. You can change them after.

To record a check, click **Record a result**, enter the date, who did it, pass or fail, and any certificate, then click **Save result**.

- A failed check takes the item out of use until a later pass.
- Staff can also click **Take out of use** with a reason, and **Put back in use** later.
- An item out of use shows **Don't use** and says why.

The [Planner](/help/planner) warns when a part needs a machine that is out of use or overdue.

## Machine inductions

The inductions table shows who has been shown how to use each machine safely, and when their refresher is due. Freelancers and others see only their own row.

Staff click **Machines & trainers** to add machines, set the refresher in months (0 means it never lapses) and choose each machine's trainers.

To record an induction, a trainer for that machine, or an admin, clicks **+ Record an induction**:

1. Pick the machine and date.
2. Pick the person, or choose **Someone else (visitor, short-term)** and type their name.
3. The trainee and the trainer both sign on screen. Click **Save induction**.

For a refresher, record a new induction. Staff or trainers can open a record and **Withdraw induction** with a reason.

Missing inductions are warnings, not blocks. The planner shows **⚠ Not inducted** when someone is planned on a machine they are not inducted on.
