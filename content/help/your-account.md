## Your menu

Click your name or photo in the top right of any page. The menu shows your name and email, and has:

- **My Profile**
- **New look (beta)**
- **Manage Users** (admins only)
- **Sign out**

## Your profile photo

Your photo shows next to your name, on tasks and in team lists.

1. Open **My Profile**.
2. Under **Profile Photo**, click **Change photo**.
3. Choose a JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF image.

The photo updates straight away. Until you add one, the Hub shows your initials.

## Changing your password

1. Open **My Profile**.
2. Under **Change Password**, enter your **Current password**.
3. Enter your **New password** twice. It must be at least 8 characters.
4. Click **Change password**.

If you have forgotten your password, you can't change it here. Ask an admin to set a new one in **Admin → Users**.

You can't change your name or email address yourself. Ask an admin.

## Notifications

The bell in the top bar shows how many unread notifications you have. The Hub checks for new ones every 30 seconds while the page is open.

Click the bell to see your notifications. Opening it marks them all as read. Click a notification to go to the page it is about. **Mark all read** clears the unread dots without opening anything.

You get notifications when, for example:

- someone assigns a task to you
- a task is waiting for your sign-off
- one of your tasks is due today or overdue

See [Tasks](/help/tasks).

## The new look

The Hub has two looks. They show the same information and the same pages; only the layout and menus differ. The new look is a beta and is off unless you or an admin turns it on.

To switch, open your menu and click **New look (beta)**. The page reloads in the other look. Click it again to go back. The setting is yours alone and stays as you left it next time you sign in.

Admins can also turn it on or off for you in **Admin → Users**.

See [Getting started](/help/getting-started) for how the two looks are laid out.

## Signing out

Open your menu and click **Sign out**. You go back to the sign-in page.
